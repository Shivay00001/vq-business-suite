FAQs — अक्सर पूछे जाने वाले सवाल

Expense tracker kaise use karein?

Har kharcha category (rent, salaries, utilities, travel, other) ke saath darj karein. App monthly totals, pie/bar charts aur budget-vs-actual report automatic banata hai.

Overspend alert kya hai?

Agar kisi category ka kharcha uske monthly budget se zyada ho jaye to alert lagta hai — taaki samay par kharchon par lagam lag sake.

Budget kaise set karein?

Neeche "Set monthly budgets" me category chunein aur monthly budget amount darj karein. Budget har mahine same rehta hai jab tak badlein nahi.

खर्च ट्रैकर क्या होता है?

Expense tracker me business ke saare kharchon ka category-wise hisaab rehta hai — isse pata chalta hai paisa kahan ja raha hai aur budget se zyada to nahi kharch ho raha.
