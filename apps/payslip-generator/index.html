Payslip generator — FAQs

पेस्लिप कैसे बनाएं?

कंपनी और कर्मचारी का विवरण, माह और वर्ष चुनें, आमदनी (एर्निंग्स) और कटौती (डिडक्शन) की पंक्तियां जोड़ें — कुल योग, नेट पे और शब्दों में राशि अपने आप बन जाएगी। फिर प्रिंट बटन दबाकर A4 पेस्लिप निकालें।

How do I create a payslip online for free in India?

Enter company and employee details, add earning rows (basic, HRA, allowances) and deduction rows (PF, ESI, TDS, professional tax). Totals, net pay and the amount in words are calculated automatically, and you can print a clean A4 payslip.

What should a salary slip contain?

Company name, employee name/ID/designation/PAN, pay month, a breakup of earnings and deductions, gross pay, total deductions, net pay and the net amount in words.

Is my payroll data uploaded anywhere?

No. Payslips are stored only in your browser's local storage under this app's private namespace.

How many free payslips do I get?

10 payslips per calendar month on the free plan. Re-viewing or re-printing a saved payslip is always free.
