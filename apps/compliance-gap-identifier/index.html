Compliance gaps — FAQs

What is a compliance gap identifier?

You enter your entity type, headcount and turnover; the tool lists the Indian statutory compliances that likely apply (GST, PF, ESI, Shops Act, Professional Tax, Udyam, TAN, ROC filings) with indicative due dates. You tick what you already do — it returns the gap list.

अनुपालन अंतर पहचानकर्ता क्या है?

अपनी इकाई का प्रकार, कर्मचारी संख्या और टर्नओवर दर्ज करें — लागू होने वाले वैधानिक अनुपालनों (GST, PF, ESI आदि) की सूची और कमी की सूची पाएं।

When does GST registration become mandatory?

Broadly at ~₹40 lakh turnover for goods (~₹20 lakh for services in most states). Inter-state supply or e-commerce can trigger it earlier. Thresholds are simplified here — confirm with your CA.

When do PF and ESI apply?

EPF broadly at 20+ employees; ESI at 10+ employees in implemented areas. Both need monthly contributions by the 15th.

Is this legal advice?

No. Thresholds are simplified guides and due dates are indicative. Confirm with your CA before registering, filing or paying.
