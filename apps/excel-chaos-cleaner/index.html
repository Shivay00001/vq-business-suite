CSV cleaner — FAQs

What does the chaos cleaner fix?

It trims whitespace, drops empty and exact-duplicate rows, renames headers to snake_case, repairs Indian-format dates (DD/MM/YYYY → YYYY-MM-DD), and strips ₹/commas from number columns.

गंदा CSV कैसे साफ़ करें?

CSV टेक्स्ट पेस्ट करें और Clean दबाएं — खाली जगह हटेगी, डुप्लिकेट पंक्तियां हटेंगी, तारीखें और नंबर ठीक होंगे, फिर साफ़ CSV डाउनलोड करें।

How are ambiguous dates like 05/06/2026 read?

Indian default: DD/MM/YYYY. So 05/06/2026 becomes 2026-06-05. Use YYYY-MM-DD in your source data to avoid ambiguity.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. Cleaning runs entirely in your browser; nothing leaves your device.

Is there a size limit?

Yes — about 0.5 MB per paste. Split bigger exports and clean them in parts.
