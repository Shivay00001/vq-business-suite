FAQs — अक्सर पूछे जाने वाले सवाल

Working capital formula kya hai?

Working capital = Current assets − Current liabilities. Positive matlab short-term me dene se zyada paane ki sthiti; negative matlab liquidity pressure.

Current ratio aur quick ratio me kya farak hai?

Current ratio = Current assets ÷ Current liabilities. Quick ratio = (Current assets − Inventory) ÷ Current liabilities — stock ko hatakar dekhta hai kyunki stock turant cash nahi banta.

Cash conversion cycle (CCC) kya hota hai?

CCC = Inventory days + Debtor days − Creditor days. Ye batata hai ki maal me laga paisa kitne dino me wapas cash banta hai. Kam CCC = behtar cash flow.

Healthy working capital kitna hona chahiye?

Thumb rule: current ratio 1.33 ya zyada, quick ratio 1 ya zyada, aur positive working capital. Lekin industry ke hisaab se farak hota hai — ye calculator thumb-rule verdict deta hai, CA ki salah ka replacement nahi.

वर्किंग कैपिटल क्या होती है?

Working capital wo paisa hai jo roz ke business chalane ke liye chahiye — current assets (cash, stock, debtors) me se current liabilities (creditors, short-term loans) ghata kar nikalti hai.
