Business restart — FAQs

What is the first thing to do when reopening a business?

Day 1 is about stabilizing: count cash and bank balances, list payables and receivables, and confirm premises access and utilities before anything else.

बंद दुकान दोबारा कैसे खोलें?

पहले दिन नकद और बैंक बैलेंस गिनें, देनदारी की सूची बनाएं, दुकान/बिजली/पानी कन्फर्म करें। फिर लाइसेंस, स्टाफ, वेंडर और अंत में दोबारा लॉन्च की घोषणा।

Temporary pause vs full shutdown — what changes?

A full shutdown adds re-registration of cancelled licenses, reactivating the bank account, and a fresh hiring round. Pick the right track at the top of the page.

When should pending tax filings be handled?

In Month 1 at the latest — pending GST/TDS filings accrue penalties, so catch up early while revenue restarts.
