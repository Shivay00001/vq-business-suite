Competitor price comparator — FAQs

How do I compare my price with competitors?

For each SKU, list competitor prices, take the average, and compute the gap % = (your price − market average) ÷ market average × 100. Within ±5% = at par; above = premium; below = budget.

कॉम्पिटिटर कीमतों से तुलना कैसे करें?

हर SKU के लिए कॉम्पिटिटर कीमतों का औसत निकालें। गैप % = (आपकी कीमत − बाज़ार औसत) ÷ बाज़ार औसत × 100। ±5% के अंदर = बराबर, ऊपर = प्रीमियम, नीचे = बजट।

When should a price gap worry me?

Beyond ±15% deserves attention: 15%+ above market needs a clear value story (brand, service, quality) or you lose share; 15%+ below may mean you are leaving money on the table.

Does premium positioning always hurt sales?

No — premium works when customers perceive extra value. The gap tells you where you stand; whether it is sustainable depends on differentiation, which this tool does not measure.

Is my pricing data private?
