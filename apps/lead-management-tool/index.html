FAQs — अक्सर पूछे जाने वाले सवाल

Lead pipeline stages ka matlab kya hai?

New = fresh lead, Contacted = baat ho gayi, Negotiating = price/terms par baat chal rahi hai, Won = deal close ho gaya, Lost = lead haath se nikal gaya. Har lead ko stage-wise aage badhayein.

Overdue follow-up highlight kyon hai?

Agar follow-up date nikal gayi aur lead abhi bhi open hai (won/lost nahi hua), to row red highlight ho jaati hai — taaki koi follow-up miss na ho.

Open pipeline value kya hoti hai?

New + Contacted + Negotiating leads ke expected values ka total — ye batata hai kitna business pipeline me hai. Won leads isme nahi judte (vo closed business hai).

लीड मैनेजमेंट क्यों ज़रूरी है?

Leads ko copy-register me track karne se follow-ups miss hote hain aur deals haath se nikal jaate hain. Pipeline view me har lead ka stage aur follow-up date saaf dikhta hai.

Kya mera data safe hai?

Haan — saara data sirf aapke browser ke vault me store hota hai, kahin upload nahi hota. Passphrase se encrypt bhi kar sakte hain.
