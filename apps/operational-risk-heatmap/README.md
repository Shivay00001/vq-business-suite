# Operational Risk Heatmap — `operational-risk-heatmap`

5×5 likelihood × impact risk matrix: register up to 25 operational risks,
score each (likelihood × impact, 1–25), band them (Low/Medium/High/Critical),
render a colour-coded heatmap grid, and rank the top 5. Register snapshots
savable to on-device vault (max 25).

## Free limits
20 risk entries/day (metered). Vault snapshots capped at 25.

## Honest limitation
Self-assessment only — scores reflect the user's own judgement. Not a
professional risk audit; page and report both say: confirm with your auditor/CA.
