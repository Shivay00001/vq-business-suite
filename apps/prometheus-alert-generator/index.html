    FAQs — अक्सर पूछे जाने वाले सवाल
    
      
    
        Prometheus alert rule kaise likhein?
        
    Alert rule me chahiye: alert name, PromQL expr (jaise up == 0), "for:" duration, severity label, aur summary/description annotations. Ye generator YAML likh deta hai.
    
      
    
      
    
        Recording rule kya hota hai?
        
    Recording rule mehengi PromQL expression ko schedule par precompute karke nayi metric banata hai (jaise job:http_errors:rate5m), taaki alerts aur dashboards sasti precomputed series query karein.
    
      
    
      
    
        "for: 5m" ka matlab kya hai?
        
    Alert tabhi fire hoga jab expression lagataar 5 minute tak true rahe — chhote spikes par flapping nahi hota.
    
      
    
      
    
        Counter metrics par alert kaise lagayein?
        
    Raw _total counter par kabhi alert na lagayein — wo sirf badhta hai. rate() use karein, jaise sum(rate(http_requests_total{status=~"5.."}[5m])) > 0.05.
