FAQs — अक्सर पूछे जाने वाले सवाल

Cron kaise set kare?

Yahan 5-field expression banayein, copy karein, phir Linux par "crontab -e" me line paste karein — jaise "*/15 * * * * /path/to/script.sh". cPanel me Cron Jobs panel use karein.

*/15 * * * * ka matlab kya hai?

Har 15 minute me — minute 0, 15, 30 aur 45 par, har ghante, har din. Ye sabse common schedules me se ek hai.

Cron me day-of-month aur day-of-week dono diye to kya hota hai?

Standard cron me dono me se koi ek match hone par job chalti hai (OR logic). Jaise "0 9 1 * 1" har mahine ki 1 tareekh AUR har Somvaar subah 9 baje chalega.

0 2 * * * ka matlab kya hai?

Roz raat 2:00 baje. Backups aur reports ke liye common timing — server load kam hota hai.
