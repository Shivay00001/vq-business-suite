Misuse risk — FAQs

What is segregation of duties?

Splitting sensitive tasks so no one person controls a whole money trail — e.g. the person who handles cash must not also record it. This tool maps each person's access and flags conflicting pairs.

कर्तव्यों का पृथक्करण क्या है?

संवेदनशील कार्यों को बांटना ताकि कोई एक व्यक्ति पूरे धन-प्रवाह को नियंत्रित न करे — जैसे नकद संभालने वाला व्यक्ति उसे दर्ज न करे।

Does a flagged conflict mean the employee is dishonest?

No. It means the role design is risky. Redesign the role — split the duties — rather than suspecting the person.

What is the most dangerous access combination?

Handling money (cash/bank) while also recording it (bookkeeping), or running payroll while controlling bank payments. Two or more conflicts on one person is High risk.

Is this an HR investigation tool?

No. It is a role-design self-assessment. Confirm changes with your auditor or HR consultant.
