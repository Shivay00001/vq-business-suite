Frequently asked questions

When can I actually pay for Pro?

Pro checkout is not live yet — the suite is in its free static phase. The reserve button above saves a local reminder on your device; we'll announce checkout on this page when it opens. Nothing is charged today.

What counts as one "use"?

Pressing calculate, or generating/saving a document (invoice, payslip, P&L). Simply opening a tool or viewing something you saved earlier never counts.

Do limits reset?

Yes — every day at midnight, automatically.

Can I cancel Pro?

Yes, any time, and you'll keep Pro until the end of the billing month. Your saved data stays on your device regardless.

Is my business data used for advertising?

No. Ads are contextual to the page, not to your data — your inputs and saved documents never leave your device.
