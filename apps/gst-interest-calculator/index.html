FAQs — अक्सर पूछे जाने वाले सवाल

GST late payment par interest kitna lagta hai?

18% per annum, Section 50(1) CGST Act ke tahat. Interest sirf net cash liability par — yaani jo tax aapne electronic cash ledger se chukaya — ITC-offset hisse par zero interest lagta hai.

Galat ITC claim karne par kitna interest hai?

24% per annum, Section 50(3) ke tahat — lekin sirf tab jab ITC galat tarike se avail aur utilise dono hui ho. Agar ITC bina use kiye credit ledger me padi rahi, to reversal par koi interest nahi.

GSTR-3B ka late fee per day kitna hai?

₹50 per day (₹25 CGST + ₹25 SGST); nil return ke liye ₹20 per day (₹10 + ₹10). Har return par maximum cap: nil ₹500 · turnover ≤ ₹1.5 crore par ₹2,000 · ₹1.5–5 crore par ₹5,000 · ₹5 crore se zyada par ₹10,000.

Interest kab se kab tak count hota hai?

Due date ke agle din se lekar actual payment (cash ledger debit) ki date tak — poore dinon ka hisaab.

Kya ye calculator ka result GST portal se match karega?

Portal Rule 88B ke tahat cash-ledger timing ko dhyan me rakhkar interest nikalta hai; ye calculator standard formula par based estimate deta hai. Final liability hamesha portal/CA se verify karein.
