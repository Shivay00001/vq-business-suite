Fraud risk indicator — FAQs

What is a fraud risk indicator?

A self-assessment checklist of common fraud red flags (fake invoices, split purchases, ghost employees). Each ticked flag adds weighted points; the total gives a fraud-risk score and the controls that close each gap.

धोखाधड़ी जोखिम संकेतक क्या है?

यह एक आत्म-मूल्यांकन सूची है — नकली चालान, बंटी हुई खरीद, फर्जी कर्मचारी जैसे संकेतों को चिह्नित करें और जोखिम स्कोर व नियंत्रण उपाय पाएं।

Does a high score mean fraud is happening?

No. A ticked flag means a control gap exists, not that fraud occurred. Treat the score as "where to strengthen controls first".

What is the single most effective anti-fraud control?

Segregation of duties: the person who handles cash or bank must not be the one who records it. Monthly bank reconciliation by an independent person comes second.

Is this legal advice or an audit?

Neither. This is a self-assessment estimate. Confirm findings with your auditor or CA before taking action against anyone.
