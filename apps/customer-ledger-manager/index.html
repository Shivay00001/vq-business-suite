FAQs — अक्सर पूछे जाने वाले सवाल

Customer ledger kaise banaye? (How to make a customer ledger?)

Customer add karein, phir har udhar bikri par Sale voucher aur jab paisa mile to Receipt voucher record karein. App automatic running balance aur aging (kitne din se baki) banata hai.

Aging 0-30 / 30-60 / 60-90 / 90+ ka matlab kya hai?

Ye batata hai ki baki (outstanding) rakam kitne din purani hai. Receipts ko FIFO se adjust kiya jata hai — pehle purani sales clear hoti hain. 90+ din ka bucket sabse risk bhara hota hai.

Khata bahi ka statement print ho sakta hai?

Haan — customer select karke Print / PDF dabayein. Statement me saari vouchers date-wise aur running balance ke saath aate hain.

Kya mera ledger data safe hai?

Haan — saara data sirf aapke device ke browser me store hota hai, kahin upload nahi hota. Upar passphrase set karke vault encrypt bhi kar sakte hain.

ग्राहक खाता बही क्या होती है?

Graahak khata bahi me har customer ke saath hue len-den ka hisaab rehta hai — kitni udhar bikri hui, kitna paisa vasool hua, aur kitna baki hai. Ye chhote vyapariyon ka sabse zaroori hisab hota hai.
