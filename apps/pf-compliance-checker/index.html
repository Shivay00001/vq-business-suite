PF compliance — FAQs

When is PF registration mandatory?

Establishments with 20 or more employees (in scheduled industries) must register with EPFO. Once the Act applies, it keeps applying even if headcount later falls below 20.

पीएफ कब अनिवार्य है?

20 या अधिक कर्मचारियों वाले प्रतिष्ठानों के लिए EPFO पंजीकरण अनिवार्य है। एक बार लागू होने के बाद, कर्मचारियों की संख्या घटने पर भी यह लागू रहता है।

What is the current PF wage ceiling?

₹25,000/month w.e.f. 17 September 2026 (S.O. 5109(E)) — raised from ₹15,000. At the ceiling, each side contributes ₹3,000/month (12%). The employer's 12% splits into 8.33% EPS (₹2,083) + 3.67% EPF (₹917), plus 0.5% EDLI and 0.5% admin charges.

By when must PF be deposited?

Contributions and the Electronic Challan-cum-Return (ECR) are due by the 15th of the following month via the EPFO Unified Portal. Late payment attracts interest and damages.

Why do UAN and KYC matter?

Every eligible employee needs a Universal Account Number (UAN); Aadhaar/bank/PAN KYC seeded against it is required for withdrawals, transfers and claims to process.
