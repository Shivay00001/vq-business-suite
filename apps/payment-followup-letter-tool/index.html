Payment follow-up — FAQs

What is the 30/60/90-day payment follow-up sequence?

First reminder at 1–29 days overdue, firm follow-up at 30–59, escalation at 60–89, and a final notice at 90+. This tool picks the right stage from the due date and drafts the letter.

When should I stop following up and take legal action?

After the 90-day final notice goes unanswered, most businesses hand the matter to counsel. A lawyer should review the final notice first.

Should the tone differ between stages?

Yes — start helpful (it may be an oversight), get firm at 30 days, escalate consequences at 60, and make the 90-day letter the final warning.
