Vendor performance scorecard — FAQs

How do I rate a vendor on a scorecard?

Enter the vendor name and month, score quality, delivery, price-competitiveness and responsiveness from 0–100 each, set your weights (total 100), and the tool gives an overall score, an A+..D grade and the trend against the previous month.

वेंडर परफॉर्मेंस स्कोरकार्ड कैसे भरें?

विक्रेता का नाम और महीना डालें, क्वालिटी, डिलीवरी, प्राइस और रिस्पॉन्सिवनेस को 0–100 में स्कोर करें, वेटेज सेट करें — कुल स्कोर, ग्रेड (A+ से D) और पिछले महीने से ट्रेंड मिलेगा।

What do the grades mean?

A+ is 90+, A is 80–89, B is 70–79, C is 60–69 and D is below 60. Grades make it easy to compare vendors at a glance.

ग्रेड का क्या मतलब है?

A+ = 90+, A = 80–89, B = 70–79, C = 60–69, D = 60 से कम।

How is the trend calculated?

The overall score is compared against the same vendor's most recent previous rating on this device. No earlier rating means no trend yet.

How much history is kept?

Up to 25 monthly ratings per vendor are kept in your browser vault on this device; the oldest is dropped. Nothing is sent anywhere.
