FAQs — अक्सर पूछे जाने वाले सवाल

Supplier ledger kaise banaye?

Supplier add karein, har kharid par Purchase voucher (due date ke saath) aur payment par Payment voucher record karein. App payable balance, aging aur due-date reminders automatic banata hai.

Due-date reminders kaise kaam karte hain?

Har purchase par due date darj karein. Payments FIFO se purani purchases me adjust hote hain. Reminders list me overdue purchases sabse upar aati hain — taaki penalty ya rishte kharab hone se pehle pay kar dein.

Customer ledger se kya farak hai?

Customer ledger me aapko paisa milna hai (receivable), supplier ledger me aapko paisa dena hai (payable). Dono ka hisaab alag rakhna zaroori hai.

सप्लायर खाता बही क्या होती है?

Supplier khata bahi me aapke suppliers ko dene wale paiso ka hisaab rehta hai — kitni kharid hui, kitna pay kiya, kitna baki hai, aur kab tak dena hai.
