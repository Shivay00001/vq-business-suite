FAQs — अक्सर पूछे जाने वाले सवाल

ITC mismatch kya hota hai?

Jab aapke purchase register me claimed ITC aur supplier ke GSTR-1 se bane GSTR-2B me available ITC me antar ho — invoice missing ho, value alag ho, ya GSTIN galat ho — use ITC mismatch kehte hain. Aisi ITC par department reversal + interest maang sakta hai.

Supplier ne invoice file nahi kiya to kya hoga? (What if the supplier didn't file GSTR-1?)

Section 16(2)(c) CGST Act ke tahat ITC tabhi milta hai jab supplier ne apne GSTR-1 me invoice report kiya ho aur tax pay kiya ho. Agar invoice 2B me nahi hai, to claimed ITC "at risk" hai — supplier se follow-up karein ya ITC reverse karke interest se bachein.

Kya ye tool GST portal se 2B download karta hai?

Nahi. Ye 100% offline tool hai — 2B aapko portal se download karke yahan paste karna hota hai. Aapka data kabhi device se bahar nahi jata.

At-risk ITC kaise calculate hota hai?

Missing-in-2B invoices par poori claimed ITC + value-difference cases me (claimed − available) ka positive antar — dono ka yog at-risk ITC hai. ₹1 tak ka antar tolerance me ignore hota hai.

Meri report ka data kahan save hota hai?

Sirf aapke browser ke local vault me (chahein to passphrase se encrypt karein). Maximum 25 reports save hoti hain.
