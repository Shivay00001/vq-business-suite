Succession planning — FAQs

What is succession planning for a small business?

Making the business transferable: documented processes, a second-in-command, diversified customers, clean books and deal-ready paperwork. This tool scores your readiness across 15 checkpoints.

सक्सेशन प्लानिंग क्या है?

बिज़नेस को हस्तांतरणीय बनाना — दस्तावेज़ी प्रक्रियाएं, सेकंड-इन-कमांड, विविध ग्राहक, साफ़ खाते। यह टूल 15 बिंदुओं पर आपकी तैयारी स्कोर करता है।

What hurts a business valuation most at exit?

Owner dependence — if the business stops when you step away for 30 days, buyers discount heavily. That is the single biggest readiness factor.

Should I sell shares or assets?

Tax and liability outcomes differ sharply. Take structuring advice from your CA and lawyer before signing anything.

Is the valuation snapshot financial advice?

No — it is a rough PAT × multiple range for the handover conversation. A registered valuer is needed for any deal.
