FAQs — अक्सर पूछे जाने वाले सवाल

Expiry date tracker kaise kaam karta hai?

Har batch ka item, batch number, mfg/expiry date aur quantity darj karein. App batches ko expired, ≤30 din aur ≤90 din buckets me baant deta hai aur risk value nikaalta hai.

FIFO issue suggestion kya hai?

Sale ke time sabse pehle expire hone wala batch pehle nikalo — ise FEFO (First Expired, First Out) kehte hain. Isse expiry loss kam hota hai.

Value at risk ka matlab kya hai?

Expired aur 30 din me expire hone wale batches ka kul cost value — ye woh paisa hai jo turant action na lene par doob sakta hai.

एक्सपायरी ट्रैकिंग क्यों ज़रूरी है?

Dawa, food aur cosmetics me expired maal bechna kanooni jurm bhi hai aur nuksaan bhi — batch-wise tracking se wastage aur risk dono kam hote hain.
