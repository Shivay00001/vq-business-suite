Subscription tracker — FAQs

How do renewal alerts work?

Enter each subscription with its renewal date. The app bands them: overdue, ≤7 days (critical), ≤15 days (warning), ≤30 days (watch), so nothing auto-renews unnoticed.

सब्सक्रिप्शन रिन्यूअल अलर्ट कैसे काम करता है?

हर सब्सक्रिप्शन की रिन्यूअल तारीख डालें। ऐप उन्हें बकाया, 7 दिन के अंदर, 15 दिन के अंदर और 30 दिन के अंदर के बैंड में दिखाता है।

How is total yearly SaaS spend calculated?

Weekly ×52, monthly ×12, quarterly ×4, half-yearly ×2 and yearly ×1. One-time purchases are excluded from the yearly total and shown separately.

Is my subscription data uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything is stored in your browser on this device via the on-device vault. Nothing is sent to any server.

How many subscriptions can I track?

Up to 25 subscriptions per device, free — 20 tracker actions per day.
