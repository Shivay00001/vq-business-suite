Fee collection tracker — FAQs

How do I track fee collection per student?

Add each student with their total fee and due date, then record payments as they come in. The ledger shows paid, due, and overdue amounts per student with a class-wise summary.

क्या बकाया फीस (overdue) अपने आप दिखेगी?

हाँ — आज की तारीख के हिसाब से जिस छात्र की due date निकल चुकी है और बकाया बचा है, वह Overdue के रूप में लाल रंग में दिखेगा, साथ में कितने दिन overdue है।

Can I record a payment bigger than the due amount?

No — the tool blocks overpayments so the ledger never goes negative. Record advance payments as a separate arrangement.

Where is the data saved?

Only in your browser via the local vault (max 25 students). Nothing leaves your device.

Does this replace my accounting software?

No — it is a simple collection ledger. For GST and statutory books, use proper accounting records and confirm with your CA.
