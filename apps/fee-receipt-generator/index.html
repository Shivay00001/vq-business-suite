Fee receipt generator — FAQs

How do I make a fee receipt for my coaching institute?

Enter the institute and student names, add fee items (tuition, admission, materials), optionally apply a discount and GST, then generate. You can print the receipt directly from the browser.

क्या कोचिंग की फीस रसीद पर GST लगाना ज़रूरी है?

शैक्षणिक संस्थानों की कुछ सेवाओं पर GST छूट है; कोचिंग/ट्रेनिंग सेवाओं पर आमतौर पर 18% GST लागू होता है। रसीद पर CGST/SGST बराबर-बराबर बँटता है। सटीक नियम के लिए अपने CA से पुष्टि करें।

Can I number my receipts automatically?

Yes — the tool suggests the next receipt number (e.g. FEE-2026-001) based on your last saved receipt. You can edit it before generating.

Where is my receipt data stored?

Only in your browser (localStorage), up to 25 saved receipts. Nothing is sent to any server.

Is this an official tax document?

It is a fee receipt printout. For GST-compliant tax invoices, follow the GST invoicing rules and confirm with your CA.
