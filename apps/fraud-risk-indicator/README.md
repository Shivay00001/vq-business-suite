# Fraud Risk Indicator — `fraud-risk-indicator`

Self-assessment red-flag checklist (12 flags: fake invoices, split
purchases, ghost employees, no segregation of duties…) with weights →
fraud-risk score % + Low/Moderate/High band + the preventive control for
each ticked gap. Assessments savable to on-device vault (max 25).

## Free limits
20 assessments/day (metered). Vault saves capped at 25.

## Honest limitation
Diagnostic, not accusatory: a ticked flag is a control gap to close, never
proof of wrongdoing. Not an audit, not legal advice — confirm with your
auditor/CA.
