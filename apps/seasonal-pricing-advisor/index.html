Seasonal pricing advisor — FAQs

What is a seasonality index?

Monthly sales ÷ average monthly sales × 100. An index of 130 means the month sells 30% above average; 70 means 30% below. It is the standard way to see your yearly rhythm.

सीज़नैलिटी इंडेक्स क्या है?

मासिक बिक्री ÷ औसत मासिक बिक्री × 100। 130 का इंडेक्स मतलब औसत से 30% ज़्यादा बिक्री, 70 मतलब 30% कम।

Should I raise prices in peak season?

Usually yes, modestly: +5% in peak months captures willingness to pay, and −5% in off-peak months can stimulate volume. Test small first — demand elasticity varies by product.

How is the revenue uplift calculated?

Same monthly volumes re-priced: peak months at base × 1.05, off-peak at base × 0.95. It assumes volume does not change with price — a simplification, so treat it as directional.
