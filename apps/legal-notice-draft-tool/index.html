Legal notice draft — FAQs

What should a legal notice for payment default contain?

Parties, invoice details, amount, due date, days overdue, a clear demand with a deadline (usually 15–30 days), and a warning of further action. This tool drafts that structure — a lawyer must review it.

Is a legal notice mandatory before filing a case?

Not always, but it strengthens recovery suits and often triggers payment. Your lawyer will advise based on the dispute type.

Can I send this draft as-is?

No. This is a template, not a filed-ready legal document. Wrong facts or wording can hurt your case — get a lawyer to review.

How long should the demand period be?

15 days is the common default; this tool allows 3–60 days. Your lawyer may adjust it.

No. This tool does not give legal advice. Consult a qualified lawyer for your situation.
