Assumptions stated on-screen

  • Normalisation: each criterion is scaled 0–1 across the vendors in this comparison. For price and lead time lower is better; for warranty and rating higher is better.
  • Score = weighted sum of normalised values (your weights), 0–100. Scores are relative to this comparison set — adding or removing a vendor changes every score.
  • Price gap is shown against the winner for context; the winner is the best weighted trade-off, not always the cheapest.
  • Ratings are your own judgement (past experience, references) — the tool does not verify them.

Vendor comparison tool — FAQs

How do I compare vendor quotes?

Enter each vendor quote — price, lead time in days, warranty in months and rating out of 5 — set your criterion weights (they must total 100), and the tool scores and ranks every vendor 0–100.

विक्रेता कोटेशन की तुलना कैसे करें?

हर विक्रेता का प्राइस, लीड टाइम (दिन), वारंटी (महीने) और रेटिंग (5 में से) डालें, वेटेज सेट करें — टूल हर विक्रेता को 0–100 स्कोर देकर रैंक करेगा।

How is the vendor score calculated?

Each criterion is normalised 0–1 across the vendors you compare: for price and lead time lower is better; for warranty and rating higher is better. The weighted sum (weights as you set them) gives the final score.

वेंडर स्कोर कैसे निकाला जाता है?

हर मानदंड को तुलना किए गए विक्रेताओं में 0–1 पर सामान्यीकृत किया जाता है: प्राइस और लीड टाइम में कम बेहतर, वारंटी और रेटिंग में ज़्यादा बेहतर। आपके वेटेज से भारित योग अंतिम स्कोर देता है।

What weights should I use?

Start with the defaults: price 40%, lead time 20%, warranty 15%, rating 25%. Raise price weight for commoditised items, lead time weight for urgent orders.

Is my comparison data stored online?

No. Up to 25 comparisons are saved in your browser vault on this device only.
