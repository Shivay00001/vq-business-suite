FAQs — अक्सर पूछे जाने वाले सवाल

Story points kya hote hain?

Story points effort/complexity ko relative terms me measure karte hain, hours me nahi. Teams aksar T-shirt sizes (XS=1 … XXL=13) ya Fibonacci numbers use karti hain taaki estimates comparative rahein.

Sprint capacity kaise calculate karein?

Average velocity (pichhle 3 sprints me complete hue points) lein aur planned leave/meetings ghata dein. Ye planner backlog ke capacity se zyada hone par warning deta hai.

Burndown chart kya dikhata hai?

Sprint days ke hisaab se bacha hua kaam. Ideal line total points se zero tak jaati hai; agar actual line uske upar rahe to scope ya pace par dhyaan dena chahiye.

Velocity aur capacity me kya antar hai?

Velocity wo hai jo aapne actually complete kiya (history); capacity wo hai jo aap lene ka plan banate hain (forecast). Healthy planning me commitment proven velocity ke paas rehta hai.
