Revenue concentration analyzer — FAQs

How do I measure customer concentration risk?

Use the Herfindahl index (HHI): square each customer's revenue share % and add them up (0–10,000 scale). Under 1,500 = diversified, 1,500–2,500 = moderate, above 2,500 = high risk.

रेवेन्यू कंसंट्रेशन रिस्क कैसे मापें?

हर ग्राहक के रेवेन्यू शेयर % का वर्ग करके जोड़ें (HHI, 0–10,000)। 1,500 से कम = विविध, 1,500–2,500 = मध्यम, 2,500 से ज़्यादा = ज़्यादा जोखिम।

What is a safe top-customer share?

A common rule: no single customer above 15–20% of revenue. Above 30% you are effectively a captive supplier — negotiate, diversify, or build switching-cost protection.

Can I use this for SKUs instead of customers?

Yes — the same HHI math works for SKU/product concentration. Pick the mode and the interpretation adapts.
