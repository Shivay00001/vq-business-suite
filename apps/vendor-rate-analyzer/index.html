Vendor rate analyzer — FAQs

How do I analyse vendor rate hikes?

Paste or upload a CSV with columns item, vendor, rate, date. The tool flags each vendor's latest rate when it is up by your threshold versus its previous quote from the same vendor or versus the item's lowest-ever rate, and names the cheapest latest vendor per item.

What CSV format is accepted?

A header row containing item, vendor, rate, date (any order), then one quote per row. Quoted fields with commas are supported. A sample is pre-filled so you can try it instantly.

CSV फॉर्मेट क्या होना चाहिए?

हेडर में item, vendor, rate, date (किसी भी क्रम में), फिर हर पंक्ति में एक कोटेशन। नमूना पहले से भरा है।

What does "best vendor" mean here?

For each item, the vendor with the cheapest most-recent quote. It does not account for quality, lead time or credit terms — use the Vendor Comparison Tool for a weighted choice.

Is my rate data stored online?

No. Up to 25 analysis snapshots are saved in your browser vault on this device only.
