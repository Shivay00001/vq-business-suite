SPOF detector — FAQs

What is a single point of failure in a business?

Any role, vendor or system whose absence stops the business: the one Tally operator, the single raw-material supplier, the billing server with no backup. This tool ranks them by dependency × coverage gap.

सिंगल पॉइंट ऑफ फेलियर क्या है?

कोई भी भूमिका, विक्रेता या सिस्टम जिसके बिना व्यवसाय रुक जाए — एकमात्र लेखाकार, एकमात्र कच्चा माल आपूर्तिकर्ता। यह टूल उन्हें निर्भरता × कवरेज अंतर से रैंक करता है।

How is the SPOF score calculated?

Score = dependency (1–5) × coverage gap (no backup = 3, partial = 2, full = 1). Max 15. Dependency 4–5 with no backup is automatically Critical.

What should I do about a Critical SPOF?

Create redundancy: cross-train a backup person, qualify an alternate vendor, or add a failover/backup for the system. Start with the top-ranked one.

Is this an operations audit?

No — it is a self-assessment estimate. Confirm your redundancy plans with your operations head or auditor.
