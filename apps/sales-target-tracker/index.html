FAQs — अक्सर पूछे जाने वाले सवाल

% complete aur gap kya hote hain?

% complete = (achieved ÷ target) × 100 — target ka kitna hissa poora ho gaya. Gap = target − achieved — target poora karne ke liye abhi kitna aur chahiye.

Month-end projection kaise nikalti hai?

Run-rate = achieved ÷ days elapsed (roz ka average). Projected = run-rate × days in month — agar isi speed se chale to month-end tak kitna hoga.

<70% alert ka matlab kya hai?

Agar koi salesperson 70% se kam complete hai to us par ⚠️ Off-track alert lagta hai — manager turant dekh sakta hai kisko push karna hai.

सेल्स टार्गेट ट्रैकिंग क्यों ज़रूरी है?

Month-end par surprise se bachne ke liye roz/beech me track karna zaroori hai. Projection se pehle hi pata chal jaata hai target miss hone wala hai ya nahi.

Kya mera data safe hai?

Haan — saara data sirf aapke browser ke vault me store hota hai. Passphrase se encrypt bhi kar sakte hain.
