Assumptions stated on-screen

  • Formula: (15 ÷ 26) × last-drawn monthly salary (Basic + DA, incl. fixed sales commission) × completed years of service.
  • Eligibility: 5 years of continuous service, except death/disablement where gratuity is payable regardless of tenure.
  • Part-year rule: service over 6 months in the final year counts as one full year; under 6 months is ignored.
  • Statutory cap: ₹20,00,000 maximum under the Act (raised from ₹10 lakh in 2018). Amounts above the cap are taxable in the employee's hands.
  • Not covered by the Act? Employers may still pay gratuity ex-gratia — the usual formula there is (15 ÷ 30) × salary × years. Confirm with your CA.
  • This is an estimate, not legal advice. Continuous-service disputes (breaks, 240-day rules) need a labour consultant — confirm with your CA before paying or filing.

Gratuity calculator — FAQs

How is gratuity calculated in India?

Gratuity = (last-drawn monthly salary × 15 × completed years of service) ÷ 26, where salary means Basic + DA. A month is taken as 26 working days, hence 15/26.

ग्रेच्युटी कैसे निकालें?

ग्रेच्युटी = (अंतिम मासिक वेतन × 15 × सेवा के पूर्ण वर्ष) ÷ 26। वेतन में बेसिक + DA शामिल है। 5 साल की निरंतर सेवा ज़रूरी है।

Who is eligible for gratuity?

Employees of establishments with 10+ workers who complete 5 years of continuous service. The 5-year rule is waived on death or disablement due to accident/disease.

Is there a maximum gratuity amount?

Yes — ₹20,00,000 under the Payment of Gratuity Act (raised from ₹10 lakh in 2018). Anything above that is taxable.

What if my company is not covered by the Act?

Employers can still pay gratuity voluntarily; the common ex-gratia formula uses 30 days instead of 26. Confirm the correct treatment with your CA.

Is this official legal advice?

No — this is an estimate using the statutory formula. Confirm with your CA or labour consultant before paying or filing.
