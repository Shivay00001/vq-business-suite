Attendance register — FAQs

How do I mark attendance for my staff?

Add each employee once, pick a date, tap P (present), A (absent), HD (half-day) or PL (paid leave) for each person, then tap “Save day's attendance”. You can mark all present with one tap and then change exceptions.

अटेंडेंस रजिस्टर में हाज़िरी कैसे लगाएँ?

कर्मचारी जोड़ें, तारीख़ चुनें, हर व्यक्ति के लिए P (उपस्थित), A (अनुपस्थित), HD (आधा दिन) या PL (सवेतन छुट्टी) दबाएँ और “Save day's attendance” दबाएँ।

How is the monthly summary calculated?

Present-equivalent days = full present days + 0.5 × half-days. Paid leave and absences are counted separately, and attendance % = present-equivalent ÷ marked days × 100.

Is my employee data private?

Yes. Nothing leaves your device. Saving is locked until you set a passphrase — data is then stored encrypted (AES-256-GCM) in this browser only. Without a passphrase, nothing is saved.

How many employees and saves are allowed?

Up to 25 employees and 20 attendance saves per day on the free plan.
