# Governance Score Engine — `governance-score-engine`

12 Yes/Partial/No questions across Board oversight, Compliance,
Transparency, Financial discipline (weighted) → 0–100 governance score,
Strong/Adequate/Needs improvement/Weak rating, and improvement actions for
dimensions under 60. Scores savable to on-device vault (max 25).

## Free limits
20 scorings/day (metered). Vault saves capped at 25.

## Honest limitation
SME self-assessment, not a statutory corporate-governance audit. Confirm
with your CA/auditor.
