Fee recovery assistant — FAQs

What are aging buckets?

Overdue dues are grouped by how late they are: 1–30, 31–60, 61–90, and 90+ days. Older buckets get firmer follow-up scripts.

प्राथमिकता स्कोर कैसे तय होता है?

0–100 का स्कोर: बकाया दिनों का 60% वज़न (120 दिन पर अधिकतम) और राशि का 40% वज़न। 70+ = critical, 40+ = high, बाकी normal। सबसे पहले critical वालों से संपर्क करें।

Does the tool send the follow-up messages?

No — it only drafts Hindi/English scripts for you to copy. You send them yourself through your own channels.

Where is the data stored?

Only in this browser session — the list is kept in memory and is not uploaded anywhere.
