# Composition Scheme Checker — `gst-composition-checker`

Eligibility verdict (eligible / not eligible + reasons) for the GST composition
scheme, plus tax estimate at 1%/5%/6% of turnover and quarterly CMP-08 amount.

## Statutory basis (verified Sep 2026)
- Sec 10(1) CGST Act + Notification 14/2019-CT: Rs 1.5 cr limit (preceding FY
  aggregate turnover); Rs 75L for Arunachal Pradesh, Manipur, Meghalaya,
  Mizoram, Nagaland, Sikkim, Tripura, Uttarakhand.
- Notification 2/2019-CT(Rate): service providers (Sec 10(2A)) 6% up to
  Rs 50L aggregate turnover.
- Rates (Rule 7): traders/manufacturers 1% (0.5+0.5), restaurants 5% (2.5+2.5),
  services 6% (3+3). No ITC; Bill of Supply only; no inter-state outward
  supply; no e-commerce TCS; no ice-cream/pan-masala/tobacco manufacture.
- CMP-08 due 18th of month after quarter; GSTR-4 annual by 30 Apr.
- The page carries an explicit "confirm with your CA" note — the verdict is
  indicative, not tax advice.

## Free limit
20 checks/day (metered).
