FAQs — अक्सर पूछे जाने वाले सवाल

Loyalty points program kaise kaam karta hai?

Aap earn rate set karte hain (jaise 1 point per ₹100). Customer ki har khareed par points judte hain. Customer chahe to points redeem karke discount pa sakta hai — 1 point = aapke set kiye redeem value (₹) ke barabar.

Points balance kaise nikalta hai?

Balance = total earned − total redeemed. Redemption value = balance × redeem-value. Redeem balance se zyada nahi ho sakta — app ise rok deti hai.

Redeem balance se zyada ho to kya hota hai?

App entry reject kar deti hai aur error dikhati hai — negative balance possible nahi hai.

लॉयल्टी प्रोग्राम क्यों ज़रूरी है?

Loyalty points se customers wapas aate hain — unhe lagta hai unki har khareed ka kuchh fayda mil raha hai. Chhoti dukano ke liye ye repeat business ka sabse sasta tareeka hai.

Kya mera data safe hai?

Haan — saara data sirf aapke browser ke vault me store hota hai. Passphrase se encrypt bhi kar sakte hain.
