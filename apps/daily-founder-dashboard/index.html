Founder dashboard — FAQs

What should a founder check every morning?

Five numbers: cash in hand, dues receivable, dues payable, net position, and open tasks. This dashboard computes them plus alerts on one screen.

फाउंडर को रोज़ सुबह क्या देखना चाहिए?

कैश, वसूलने योग्य बकाया, देय बकाया, नेट पोज़िशन और खुले टास्क — एक ही स्क्रीन पर अलर्ट के साथ।

Are my dashboard numbers stored anywhere?

No — cash and dues numbers are computed in your browser and never leave it. Only your task list is saved on-device (max 25 tasks).

What does a critical alert mean?

Payables exceed cash in hand, or zero cash with dues moving. Treat those days as collection days first.

Is this financial advice?

No — a morning visibility tool. Big money decisions still go through your CA.
