Frequently asked questions

Is the Business Suite really free?

Yes. Every tool is free with generous daily limits, supported by ads. Pro (₹299/month, launching soon) raises limits and removes ads.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your own device, in your browser's local storage. Nothing is uploaded to any server. You can export or delete it any time, and optionally encrypt sensitive items with a passphrase.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Profiles are local labels (your name and business) so saved documents can be personalised — there are no passwords and no signups.

Are the GST and tax calculations accurate?

Tools implement standard published formulas and show them on-screen with their assumptions. They are a convenience, not professional tax advice — verify important figures with your CA.

Will there be an API for developers?

Yes — an Enterprise API tier is planned. Draft documentation is already published on the API docs page.
