Bank reconciliation — FAQs

How does this bank reconciliation tool match entries?

Paste your bank statement and your book (cash-book) entries as date, description, amount rows. The tool matches rows with the same sign whose amounts agree within your rupee tolerance and whose dates agree within your day tolerance.

बैंक रिकंसिलिएशन कैसे करें?

बैंक स्टेटमेंट और अपनी किताब की एंट्रियों को date, description, amount के रूप में पेस्ट करें। टूल राशि और तारीख की सहनशीलता (tolerance) के अंदर मिलान करता है और बेमेल एंट्रियाँ अलग सूची में दिखाता है।

What sign convention should the amounts use?

Use the same convention in both files: receipts/credits positive, payments/debits negative. Mixed conventions will not match.

What do unmatched entries usually mean?

Unmatched bank rows are typically bank charges, interest, or direct credits missing from your books. Unmatched book rows are typically unpresented cheques or entries the bank has not processed yet.

Is my statement data uploaded anywhere?

No — everything runs in your browser on your device. Nothing is sent to any server.
