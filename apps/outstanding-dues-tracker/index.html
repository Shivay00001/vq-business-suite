FAQs — अक्सर पूछे जाने वाले सवाल

Collection priority ranking kya hai?

Priority = days overdue × outstanding amount. Jo invoice zyada din se baki hai aur jiski rakam badi hai, wo list me upar aata hai — taaki vasuli ki koshish sabse pehle wahin ho.

Kya app WhatsApp par reminder bhej deta hai?

Nahi. App sirf ek polite reminder text taiyaar karta hai. Aap use copy karke apne WhatsApp se khud bhejte hain — koi auto-send nahi hota.

Days overdue kaise nikala jata hai?

Aaj ki date − due date. Agar due date beet gayi hai to din gin kar dikhaya jata hai; jo invoices abhi due nahi hui, un par "X days left" dikhta hai.

बकाया वसूली में ये ऐप कैसे मदद करता है?

Har invoice ka baki paisa, kitne din se overdue hai, aur kisse pehle vasool karna hai — ye sab ek list me. Saath me taiyaar WhatsApp reminder text taaki customer ko politely yaad dilaya ja sake.
