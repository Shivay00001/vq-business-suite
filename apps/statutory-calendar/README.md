# Statutory Compliance Calendar — `statutory-calendar`

Month view of recurring statutory due dates with per-law filters (saved to
vault) and a next-7-days upcoming widget. Professional Tax is state-specific,
so instead of a fixed date the calendar offers a **user-configurable PT
reminder** (state + day of month), saved in the vault and rendered as a
monthly reminder event.

## Dates used (verified Sep 2026)
GSTR-1 11th · GSTR-3B 20th · GSTR-7/8 10th · IFF & GSTR-6 13th · CMP-08 18th
(Jan/Apr/Jul/Oct) · GSTR-4 30 Jun (FY 2024-25 onwards, Notification 12/2024) · QRMP GSTR-1 13th quarterly · QRMP GSTR-3B
22nd/24th · PMT-06 25th · TDS deposit 7th · 24Q 31 Jul/31 Oct/31 Jan/31 May ·
Form 16 15 Jun · PF & ESI 15th · advance tax 15 Jun/15 Sep/15 Dec/15 Mar ·
GSTR-9/9C 31 Dec. PT dates are state-specific — deliberately not rendered;
the page says so.

## Honest note
Dates are standard recurring dates; extensions happen via notification — the
page tells users to re-verify near deadlines.

## Free limit
None — unlimited (calendar reading is never metered).
