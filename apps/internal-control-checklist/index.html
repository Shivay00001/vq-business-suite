Internal control checklist — FAQs

What is an internal control checklist?

A list of preventive controls across purchase, sales, cash & bank, inventory, and HR & payroll. You tick the ones you actually follow; the tool reports compliance % per process and a gap list.

आंतरिक नियंत्रण चेकलिस्ट क्या है?

खरीद, बिक्री, नकद-बैंक, स्टॉक और HR-वेतन में नियंत्रणों की सूची। जो नियंत्रण आप सच में अपनाते हैं उन्हें चिह्नित करें — प्रक्रिया-वार अनुपालन % और कमी की सूची पाएं।

What is a good internal control compliance score?

80%+ is strong, 60–79% adequate, 40–59% weak, below 40% means critical gaps. Fix cash & bank and purchase gaps first — that is where losses hurt most.

Is this an internal audit?

No — it is a self-assessment to prioritise control fixes. A real internal audit also tests whether controls actually work, not just whether they exist on paper.

How often should I redo this checklist?

Quarterly, or whenever staff roles change. Controls decay silently — the person who was independent last year may now do both jobs.
