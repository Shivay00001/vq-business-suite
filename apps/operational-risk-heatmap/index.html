Risk heatmap — FAQs

What is a 5×5 risk heatmap?

A 5×5 risk heatmap plots each risk by likelihood (1 = Rare to 5 = Almost certain) against impact (1 = Insignificant to 5 = Catastrophic). The score is likelihood × impact, from 1 to 25.

रिस्क हीटमैप क्या है?

रिस्क हीटमैप में हर जोखिम को संभावना (1–5) और प्रभाव (1–5) के आधार पर रखा जाता है। स्कोर = संभावना × प्रभाव। यह आत्म-मूल्यांकन का उपकरण है।

How is the risk score calculated?

Risk score = likelihood rating × impact rating. Bands: 1–4 Low, 5–9 Medium, 10–15 High, 16–25 Critical.

What do the risk bands mean?

Low: monitor. Medium: review controls periodically. High: needs a mitigation plan and an owner. Critical: investigate first — the business could be seriously harmed.

How many risks can I register?

Up to 25 risks per register, with up to 25 saved snapshots on your device.

Is this a substitute for a professional risk audit?

No — it is a self-assessment to help you prioritise. Confirm your risk treatment with your auditor or CA before acting on it.
