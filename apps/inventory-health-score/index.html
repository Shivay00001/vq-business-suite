Formula — स्कोर कैसे बनता है

Har component 0–25 points, total 0–100:

Stockouts: 25 − (5 × incidents) (min 0) — har stockout incident 5 points kaat-ta hai

Dead stock: max(0, 25 − dead%) — har 1% dead stock = 1 point kam

Near-expiry: max(0, 25 − nearExpiry%) — har 1% near-expiry value = 1 point kam

Negative stock: 25 − (8 × flags) (min 0) — har negative-balance item 8 points kaat-ta hai

Grades: A ≥ 85 · B ≥ 70 · C ≥ 50 · D < 50

Saved data padhna free hai — sirf report snapshot save karne par daily limit (25) lagti hai.

FAQs — अक्सर पूछे जाने वाले सवाल

Inventory health score kya batata hai?

Ye 0–100 ka composite score hai jo 4 cheezon se banta hai — stockouts, dead stock %, near-expiry value %, aur negative stock flags. Score jitna zyada, inventory utni healthy.

Grade A/B/C/D ka matlab kya hai?

A (85+) excellent, B (70–84) good, C (50–69) needs attention, D (below 50) urgent action needed — har grade ke saath improvement tips milte hain.

Data auto-detect kaise hota hai?

Agar aapne Stock Register, Dead Stock Analyzer ya Expiry Date Tracker apps me data daala hai to wahan se dead-stock %, near-expiry % aur negative flags automatic liye jaate hain. Stockout incidents manual hi dene hote hain.

इन्वेंटरी हेल्थ स्कोर क्यों ज़रूरी है?

Ek number me poori inventory ki sehat — kahan paisa fasa hai, kahan stockout ka khatra hai, ye sab ek glance me pata chalta hai.
