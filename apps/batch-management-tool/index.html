Batch management tool — FAQs

How do I create a batch?

Enter the batch name, capacity, schedule days, timing and fee plan, then press Add. Each batch tracks its own enrollments.

बैच फुल होने पर क्या होता है?

क्षमता से ज़्यादा नामांकन ब्लॉक हो जाता है और FULL चेतावनी दिखती है। 80% भरने पर "nearly full" अलर्ट मिलता है ताकि आप नया बैच खोल सकें।

How is expected revenue calculated?

Enrolled students × batch fee. It is a planning estimate, not collected cash.

Where is batch data stored?

Only in your browser vault (max 25 batches). Nothing is uploaded.
