FAQs — अक्सर पूछे जाने वाले सवाल

Customer database me kya-kya record karna chahiye?

Har customer ke liye name, phone, address, GSTIN aur tags/segments (jaise retail, vip, dealer) record karein. GSTIN business customers ke liye zaroori hai; tags se aap baad me customers ko segment karke target kar sakte hain.

Tags ya segments ka fayda kya hai?

Tags se aap customers ko groups me baant sakte hain — jaise vip, dealer, retail. Filter se turant us segment ke saare customers nikaal sakte hain, jo marketing aur follow-ups me kaam aata hai.

Kya customer list export ho sakti hai?

Haan — Export CSV dabane par poori customer list CSV file me download hoti hai, jo Excel ya Google Sheets me khul jaati hai. Free me 20 exports/day milte hain.

ग्राहक डेटाबेस क्या होता है?

Graahak database me aapke saare customers ka master record rehta hai — naam, phone, pata, GSTIN aur tags. Ek jagah organised data se billing, follow-up aur marketing sab aasaan ho jaata hai.

Kya mera data safe hai?

Haan — saara data sirf aapke browser ke vault me store hota hai, kahin upload nahi hota. Passphrase set karke ise encrypt bhi kar sakte hain.
