Assumptions stated on-screen

  • Linear additive model: criteria are independent and trade off linearly — standard for screening decisions, not for complex interactions.
  • Weights sum to exactly 1.00 — the tool refuses to score otherwise, so weights always mean "share of importance".
  • Scores 1–10 are your judgement — the engine structures it, it does not replace it.
  • Sensitivity ±20% per weight, others renormalised proportionally.
  • Decision aid only — not financial advice.

Decision support engine — FAQs

How does weighted scoring help a business decision?

List criteria (cost, upside, risk, effort), weight them to sum to 1, score each option 1–10 per criterion. The weighted total ranks options transparently — anyone can see which criterion drove the result.

वेटेड स्कोरिंग से फ़ैसला कैसे लें?

मानदंड तय करें, वज़न दें (योग 1), हर विकल्प को 1–10 स्कोर दें। वेटेड स्कोर से पारदर्शी रैंकिंग मिलती है।

What is the sensitivity check?

Each criterion weight is moved ±20% (others renormalised) and the ranking re-run. If the winner never changes, the recommendation is robust; if it flips, those weights need a second look before you commit.

Why must weights sum to 1?

So scores stay on the 1–10 scale and weights mean "share of importance". The tool refuses to score until the sum is exactly 1.00.
