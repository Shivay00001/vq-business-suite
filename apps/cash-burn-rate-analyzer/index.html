Burn analysis

Assumptions stated on-screen

  • Net burn = (first balance − last balance) ÷ (months − 1), i.e. the average monthly cash decrease.
  • Runway = latest balance ÷ net burn. Zero-cash month = latest month + runway (rounded up).
  • Assumes the average burn continues unchanged — seasonality, one-off expenses and new funding are not modelled.
  • Cash-flow positive businesses get no burn and no zero-cash date.
  • Simple projection, not financial advice — review monthly; confirm with your CA.

Cash burn — FAQs

What is cash burn rate?

The average monthly decrease in your cash balance. If you had ₹10 lakh in January and ₹7 lakh in April, your net burn is ₹1 lakh per month.

कैश बर्न रेट क्या है?

आपके कैश बैलेंस में औसत मासिक कमी। रनवे = मौजूदा कैश ÷ मासिक बर्न — यह बताता है कि पैसा कितने महीने चलेगा।

What is runway?

Runway = current cash ÷ monthly net burn. It is the number of months you can operate at the current burn before cash hits zero.

How many months of balances should I enter?

At least 2; 6–12 months gives a steadier average that smooths one-off spikes.

My balance is growing — what does the tool say?

It reports you as cash-flow positive with no burn and no zero-cash date, and still recommends a 3–6 month emergency reserve.

Is this a forecast?

A simple projection, not a forecast — it assumes the average burn continues. Seasonality and new funding are not modelled.
