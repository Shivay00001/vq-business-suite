Cheque management — FAQs

How long is a cheque valid in India?

3 months from the date of issue, per the RBI circular effective 1 April 2012. The same applies to demand drafts, pay orders and banker's cheques. Presented later, the bank dishonours it as stale.

चेक कितने समय तक वैध रहता है?

जारी होने की तारीख से 3 महीने तक। इसके बाद चेक stale हो जाता है और बैंक भुगतान नहीं करता।

What happens to a stale cheque?

The bank will not honour it. Ask the drawer to cancel it and issue a fresh instrument; do not simply alter the date.

Does the tool warn before a cheque expires?

Yes — pending instruments expiring within 7 days get an "expiring" alert, and instruments past 90 days get a STALE alert.

Where is my cheque register stored?

On your own device (encrypted in your browser). Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Can I track received cheques awaiting deposit too?

Yes — mark direction as "received" and status "pending"; the pending-encashment totals show money not yet in your account.
