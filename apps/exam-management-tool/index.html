Exam management tool — FAQs

How do I build a datesheet?

Add one paper at a time: class, subject, date, start/end time and hall. The tool blocks two papers for the same class at overlapping times.

डेटशीट में एक ही कक्षा के दो पेपर एक साथ आ गए तो?

टूल ऐसी एंट्री रोक देता है — एक ही कक्षा के दो पेपर एक ही दिन एक ही समय पर नहीं रखे जा सकते।

How does hall allocation work?

Enter total students and each hall/room capacity. Students fill rooms in order; the tool tells you how many are seated and whether you are short of seats.

What is in the admit-card list?

Enter roll numbers and names per class, and the tool generates the admit-card data: roll, name, class and the full paper schedule.

Is this an official board datesheet?

No — it is a planning tool for your institute. Board exams follow the board's own datesheet.
