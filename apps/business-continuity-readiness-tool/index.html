Continuity readiness — FAQs

What is business continuity readiness?

How quickly your business could keep running after a disruption — fire, flood, server failure, or a key person falling ill. This tool scores 12 readiness items (backups, tested restores, alternate site, key-person cover, insurance) into a readiness %.

व्यवसाय निरंतरता तत्परता क्या है?

आग, बाढ़, सर्वर खराबी या किसी प्रमुख व्यक्ति के बीमार पड़ने के बाद आपका व्यवसाय कितनी जल्दी चलता रह सकता है — इसका आत्म-मूल्यांकन।

What is the most neglected BCP item?

Tested restores. Most businesses back up but never verify the backup actually works — then discover it is corrupt on the day they need it.

Do small businesses need a continuity plan?

Yes — a one-page version. Write down: where work continues, who covers key roles, how to reach the insurer and CA, and where the backups are. That covers 80% of it.

Is this a certified BCP audit?

No. It is a self-assessment estimate. Confirm your continuity plan with your auditor or insurer.
