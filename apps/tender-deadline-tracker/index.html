Assumptions stated on-screen

  • Urgency bands: overdue = past deadline; critical = ≤3 days left; due soon = 4–7 days; normal = 8+ days. Days are whole calendar days from today.
  • Deadline time: portals (GeM, e-procure) close at the exact time stated in the bid document — enter and watch the time too, not just the date.
  • Late bids: are summarily rejected on GeM and in most government tenders — no grace period.
  • EMD: typically 1–2% of the estimated contract value (check the NIT); MSE/Startup-registered bidders are often exempt via Bid Securing Declaration; refunded to unsuccessful bidders without interest, typically within 30 days of award.
  • This is a planning aid, not legal advice. Always verify dates against the corrigenda/NIT — deadlines change. Confirm with your CA/consultant before bidding.

Tender deadline tracker — FAQs

How do I track tender submission deadlines?

Add each tender with its submission deadline from the NIT/GeM bid document. The tracker computes days left and color-codes urgency: red for overdue, orange for ≤3 days, amber for ≤7 days.

टेंडर डेडलाइन कैसे ट्रैक करें?

NIT या GeM दस्तावेज़ से सबमिशन डेडलाइन डालें। टूल बचे हुए दिन निकालकर रंग-कोड दिखाता है: लाल (अतिदेय), नारंगी (≤3 दिन), पीला (≤7 दिन)।

What happens if I miss a tender submission deadline?

Late bids are summarily rejected on GeM and in most government tenders — portals close exactly at the deadline time. Track both date and time from the bid document.

EMD कैसे ट्रैक करें?

हर टेंडर के साथ EMD राशि दर्ज करें। MSE/Startup पंजीकृत बोलीदाता कई टेंडरों में EMD से छूट पाते हैं (Bid Securing Declaration) — NIT ज़रूर देखें।

Is my tender data sent to any server?
