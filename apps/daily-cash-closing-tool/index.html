Daily cash closing — FAQs

How does the daily cash closing work?

Enter the count of each note and coin denomination. The tool totals the counted cash and compares it with the expected closing balance (opening + cash sales − cash payouts) to flag shortage or excess.

रोज़ का कैश क्लोज़िंग कैसे करें?

हर नोट/सिक्के की गिनती दर्ज करें। टूल कुल नकदी निकालकर अपेक्षित बैलेंस से मिलान करता है — कमी (shortage) या अधिकता (excess) तुरंत दिखती है।

What should I do if there is a shortage?

Re-count first, then check for unrecorded payouts, petty-cash slips and data-entry errors. Log the difference with a note so the pattern is visible over days.

Are ₹2000 notes included?

Yes — the ₹2000 note remains legal tender, so it is part of the count.

Where is the closing log stored?

On your own device (up to 25 entries). Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Does tallying replace my cashbook?

No — it is a daily control check. Keep your cashbook entries; this tool verifies the physical cash matches them.
