FAQs — अक्सर पूछे जाने वाले सवाल

Free GST invoice kaise banaye?

Seller aur buyer details bharein, items add karein (HSN/SAC, quantity, rate, GST%), same-state ya other-state chunein — tax automatic calculate hoga. Save karke Print/PDF lein. 10 invoices/month free.

CGST/SGST aur IGST me kya farak hai?

Same state me sale par tax aadha CGST + aadha SGST hota hai; doosre state me sale par poora IGST. Toggle badalte hi calculation update ho jata hai.

GST 2.0 ke baad kaunse rates lagu hain?

56th GST Council (September 2025) ke baad mukhya slabs 5% aur 18% hain, luxury/sin goods par 40% demerit rate — 22 September 2025 se effective. Purane 12%/28% legacy option me uplabdh hain.

Invoice number automatic kaise banta hai?

Har save par number auto-generate hota hai (INV-FY-0001 format) aur counter aapke device par save rehta hai, taaki numbering kabhi repeat na ho.

Kya mera invoice data safe hai?

Haan — invoices sirf aapke browser ke local storage me save hote hain, kahin upload nahi hote. Chahein to Vault passphrase set karke encrypt bhi kar sakte hain.
