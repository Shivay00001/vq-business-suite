Business valuation — FAQs

How is a small business valued in India?

Common methods: revenue multiple (0.5–2x annual revenue), profit multiple (3–6x PAT), and discounted cash flow. This tool gives a range across all three — labelled an ESTIMATE.

बिज़नेस की वैल्यूएशन कैसे निकालें?

तीन तरीकों से — रेवेन्यू मल्टिपल, प्रॉफिट मल्टिपल और DCF — रेंज के रूप में। यह केवल अनुमान (ESTIMATE) है, फाइनेंशियल एडवाइस नहीं।

What multiple should I use for revenue?

Indian SMEs typically trade at 0.5–2x annual revenue depending on growth, margins and assets. This tool adjusts the multiple band by your growth rate.

What is DCF-lite?

A simplified discounted cash flow: your free cash flow projected for up to 15 years and discounted to today. Terminal value is excluded, so it is conservative.

Is this official financial advice?

No — a negotiation starting point. Selling or raising money needs a registered valuer and due diligence.
