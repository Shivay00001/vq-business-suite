EMI calculator — FAQs

How is EMI calculated in India?

EMI is calculated on a reducing-balance basis: EMI = P × r × (1+r)n / ((1+r)n − 1), where P is the loan amount, r the monthly rate (annual ÷ 12 ÷ 100) and n the tenure in months. This tracker uses exactly that formula.

लोन EMI कैसे निकालें?

ऊपर फॉर्म में लोन राशि, वार्षिक ब्याज दर और अवधि डालें — कैलकुलेट बटन दबाते ही मासिक EMI, कुल ब्याज और पूरी अमोर्टाइज़ेशन शेड्यूल मिल जाएगी।

Does a prepayment reduce my EMI or my tenure?

Both are possible. Choose "Reduce tenure" to keep the EMI and finish earlier (usually saves more interest), or "Reduce EMI" to lower the monthly outgo for the same tenure. The impact box shows months saved and interest saved.

Is my loan data stored online?

No. Saved loans stay in your own browser's local storage under this app's private namespace. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Is this financial advice?

No — a general-purpose calculator. Actual bank EMIs can differ slightly due to rounding, disbursement dates and floating rates; confirm with your lender.
