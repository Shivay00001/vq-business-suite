FAQs — अक्सर पूछे जाने वाले सवाल

Referral status ka flow kya hai?

Invited = referee ko invite kiya gaya, Joined = vo jud gaya (signup/visit), Purchased = usne khareed liya. Reward sirf purchased par due hota hai.

Reward due aur reward paid me kya farak hai?

Due = referee ne purchase kar liya par referrer ko reward abhi dena baaki hai. Log me Mark paid dabane par vo paid me chala jaata hai.

Top referrers list kaise banti hai?

Har referrer ke purchased referrals gine jaate hain — sabse zyada successful referrals wala sabse upar. Ye aapke best brand ambassadors hain.

रेफरल प्रोग्राम क्यों ज़रूरी है?

Referral se aane wale customers ka bharosa pehle se hota hai aur vo aam leads se zyada khareedte hain. Chhota sa reward dekar aap apne customers ko apna sales team bana sakte hain.

Kya mera data safe hai?

Haan — saara data sirf aapke browser ke vault me store hota hai. Passphrase se encrypt bhi kar sakte hain.
