FAQs — अक्सर पूछे जाने वाले सवाल

Cashbook kaise banaye? (रोकड़ बही कैसे बनाएं?)

Opening balance set karein, phir roz ki cash in/out entries category aur note ke saath darj karein. App automatic day-wise closing balance nikalta hai.

Cash-vs-book difference ka matlab kya hai?

Book balance (hisab) aur tijori me gine hue cash me farak hona chahiye zero. Agar farak hai to koi entry chhoot gayi hai ya galti hui hai — ye check usi ko pakadta hai.

Month view me kya dikhta hai?

Har din ka total cash in, cash out aur us din ka closing balance — poore mahine ka cash flow ek nazar me.

कैश बुक क्या होती है?

Cashbook (rokad bahi) me roz ke nakad len-den ka hisaab rehta hai — kitna cash aaya, kitna gaya, aur din ke ant me kitna bacha. Chhote dukaandaaron ka sabse zaroori register.
