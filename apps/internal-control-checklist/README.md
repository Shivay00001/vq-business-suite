# Internal Control Checklist — `internal-control-checklist`

17-control library across Purchase (4), Sales (3), Cash & Bank (4),
Inventory (3), HR & Payroll (3). Tick what you actually follow →
overall compliance %, per-process scores with bars, and a gap list.
Assessments savable to on-device vault (max 25).

## Free limits
20 evaluations/day (metered). Vault saves capped at 25.

## Honest limitation
Self-assessment, not an internal audit: it checks existence of controls,
not whether they work. Confirm with your auditor.
