# Business Restart Toolkit

Step-by-step reopening checklist after a crash/shutdown: Day 1 / Week 1 / Month 1 phases (licenses, staff, vendors, cash, relaunch). Two tracks: temporarily paused vs fully shut down.

- **Cluster:** platform-lifecycle (Cluster N)
- **Slug:** `business-restart-toolkit`
- **Files:** `index.html`, `app.js`
- **Storage:** none (stateless). **Metering:** 20 progress checks/day.

Pure API: `stepsFor`, `phasesFor`, `progress`, `esc`.
