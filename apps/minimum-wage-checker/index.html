Minimum wage — FAQs

Is there one national minimum wage in India?

No. States fix their own minimum wages by skill level, zone and scheduled employment; the Code on Wages, 2019 adds a central floor wage as a baseline. Always check your state's notification.

क्या पूरे भारत में एक न्यूनतम वेतन है?

नहीं। राज्य कौशल स्तर, ज़ोन और निर्धारित नियोजन के अनुसार अपने न्यूनतम वेतन तय करते हैं। हमेशा अपने राज्य की अधिसूचना जाँचें।

What is VDA and how often do rates change?

Variable Dearness Allowance (VDA) is the inflation-linked part of the wage, revised usually every six months (1 April and 1 October in the central sphere). The wage you check today may change next quarter.

Why does this checker cover only some states?

Because we only include values we could verify directly from a cited source, with effective dates. Unverified or OCR-unclear values are deliberately excluded rather than guessed.

What is the penalty for paying below minimum wage?

Under the Code on Wages, paying below the notified minimum wage can lead to imprisonment up to three months and/or a fine up to ₹1,00,000, plus payment of arrears.

Is this official compliance advice?

No — this is a self-check. Confirm the latest notification of your state labour department before relying on it.
