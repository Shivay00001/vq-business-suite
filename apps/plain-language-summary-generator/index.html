Plain-language summary — FAQs

What does this plain-language tool do?

Paste a jargon-heavy paragraph — a tax notice, an audit note — and it pulls out the key numbers (amounts, rates, deadlines) and rewrites the important sentences in plain words.

कठिन कानूनी भाषा को आसान कैसे समझें?

पैराग्राफ पेस्ट करें — टूल मुख्य रकम, दरें और समय-सीमा निकालकर ज़रूरी बातों को सरल भाषा में बुलेट पॉइंट में देता है।

Does it interpret the law for me?

No. It simplifies wording and surfaces numbers; it does not interpret legal meaning or give advice. A CA or lawyer must interpret notices.

What text works best?

One focused paragraph (a notice, an audit observation, a clause). Very long documents should be pasted one key section at a time.

Is my pasted text stored anywhere?

No — everything is processed in your browser and nothing is uploaded or saved.
