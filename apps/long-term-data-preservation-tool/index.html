Data preservation — FAQs

How long must books of account be kept in India?

A company must preserve books of account and vouchers for at least 8 financial years (Companies Act, 2013, Sec 128(5)); income-tax books for 6 years from the end of the relevant AY; GST records for 72 months from the annual-return due date.

भारत में रिकॉर्ड कितने साल रखने होते हैं?

कंपनी की बही-खाते कम से कम 8 वित्तीय वर्ष (कंपनी अधिनियम 2013, धारा 128(5)); आयकर बही संबंधित निर्धारण वर्ष के अंत से 6 वर्ष; GST रिकॉर्ड वार्षिक रिटर्न की देय तिथि से 72 महीने।

Can I destroy records as soon as the period ends?

Only after confirming no litigation, appeal, assessment or investigation is open that needs them. This tool flags eligibility for review — the destruction decision stays with you and your CA.
