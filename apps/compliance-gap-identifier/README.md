# Compliance Gap Identifier — `compliance-gap-identifier`

Entity-type based statutory checklist: enter entity type, headcount,
turnover and premises → simplified applicability engine for GST, Shops
& Establishments, EPF, ESI, Professional Tax, TAN/TDS, Udyam, trade
licence and ROC filings, each with indicative due dates. Tick what you
already do → compliance coverage % and gap list. Reports savable to
on-device vault (max 25).

## Free limits
20 checks/day (metered). Vault saves capped at 25.

## Honest limitation
Thresholds are simplified guides (not legal definitions); due dates are
indicative; state rules vary. Confirm with your CA.
