Delayed payment interest — FAQs

What interest applies on delayed payments to MSME suppliers?

Under MSMED Act Section 16, the buyer must pay compound interest with monthly rests at three times the RBI bank rate, from the appointed day until payment — regardless of any contrary agreement.

What is the current RBI bank rate used here?

The default is 5.50% p.a., the RBI Bank Rate verified in September 2026 (repo 5.25%). That makes the Section 16 rate 16.50% p.a. The rate is editable — always re-check the latest RBI notification before filing a claim.

Within how many days must a buyer pay an MSME supplier?

Section 15: on or before the agreed date in writing, and in no case later than 45 days from acceptance of goods/services. Interest runs from the appointed day (15 days after acceptance) when no date is agreed.

Can I claim interest even if the principal was paid late?

Yes — interest can be claimed on its own even if the principal was eventually paid. File through the MSME Samadhaan portal / Facilitation Council of the supplier's state.
