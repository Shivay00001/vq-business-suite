Product profit analyzer — FAQs

How do I find my most profitable product?

For each SKU compute gross profit (revenue − COGS) and margin % (gross ÷ revenue). Rank by gross profit rupees — the SKU contributing the most rupees is your best, regardless of its margin %.

सबसे ज़्यादा मुनाफ़ेवाला प्रोडक्ट कैसे पता करें?

हर SKU का ग्रॉस प्रॉफ़िट (रेवेन्यू − COGS) और मार्जिन % निकालें। रुपयों में सबसे ज़्यादा योगदान देने वाला SKU सबसे अच्छा है — मार्जिन % चाहे जो हो।

Margin % vs contribution share — which matters?

Margin % tells efficiency; contribution share tells rupees. A low-margin, high-volume SKU can fund the business while a high-margin niche SKU cannot — rank by gross rupees first.

What if a product has negative margin?

It destroys value on every sale. Either reprice, cut its cost, or drop it — unless it is a deliberate loss-leader with measured cross-sell, which this tool flags for review.
