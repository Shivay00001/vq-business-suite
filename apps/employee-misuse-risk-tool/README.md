# Employee Misuse Risk Tool — `employee-misuse-risk-tool`

Access-vs-role matrix checker: up to 25 staff mapped against 6 access
types (cash, bank, stock, purchases, payroll, bookkeeping). Flags 6
segregation-of-duties conflict pairs, assigns High/Medium/Review/Low
risk, ranks worst-first. Snapshots savable to on-device vault (max 25).

## Free limits
20 entries/day (metered). Vault snapshots capped at 25.

## Honest limitation
Role-design self-assessment, not an HR investigation: a conflict means
the ROLE needs redesigning, never a judgement on the person. Confirm
with your auditor/HR consultant.
