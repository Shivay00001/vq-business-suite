ESI compliance — FAQs

When is ESI registration mandatory?

Non-seasonal factories/establishments with 10 or more employees in an ESI-notified area must register within 15 days of becoming eligible (20 in some states for certain establishments). Once registered, coverage continues even if headcount drops.

ईएसआई पंजीकरण कब अनिवार्य है?

ईएसआई-लागू क्षेत्र में 10 या अधिक कर्मचारियों वाले कारखानों/प्रतिष्ठानों के लिए पंजीकरण अनिवार्य है। एक बार पंजीकृत होने पर, संख्या घटने पर भी कवरेज जारी रहता है।

What are the ESI contribution rates?

0.75% of gross wages from the employee and 3.25% from the employer — 4% total, unchanged since July 2019. Employees with average daily wages up to ₹176 are exempt from the employee share (the employer still pays its part).

Who is covered under ESI?

Employees with gross monthly wages up to ₹21,000 (₹25,000 for persons with disabilities). Once someone crosses the ceiling, they exit from the next contribution period.

By when must ESI be deposited?

By the 15th of the following month through the ESIC portal, with half-yearly returns due 11 November (Apr–Sep) and 12 May (Oct–Mar).
