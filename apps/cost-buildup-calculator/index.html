Assumptions stated on-screen

  • GST treatment: GST on inputs is excluded from cost when ITC is claimable (regular registered dealer). It is added to cost for composition dealers, exempt supplies, or blocked credits.
  • Suggested price uses margin-on-price: price = landed cost ÷ (1 − margin). A 30% margin needs price = cost ÷ 0.70, not cost × 1.30.
  • Not included: output GST on your sale, commissions, marketplace fees, wastage beyond your inputs — add them as overhead if they apply.

Cost buildup calculator — FAQs

How do I calculate landed cost per unit?

Add material + labour + overhead + freight to get total pre-GST cost. If input tax credit (ITC) is claimable, GST on inputs is not a cost; if not claimable, add it. Divide the landed total by the number of units.

लैंडेड कॉस्ट प्रति यूनिट कैसे निकालें?

मैटेरियल + लेबर + ओवरहेड + फ्रेट जोड़ें = प्री-GST लागत। ITC मिलता है तो GST लागत नहीं, नहीं मिलता तो जोड़ें। कुल को यूनिट्स से भाग दें।

Is GST on inputs part of product cost?

Only when you cannot claim input tax credit — e.g. composition dealers, exempt supplies, or personal-use inputs. Regular GST-registered businesses claim ITC, so GST is not a cost.

How do I set a selling price from landed cost?

Price = landed cost per unit ÷ (1 − target margin). For a 30% margin on price with ₹73.16 landed cost: 73.16 ÷ 0.70 = ₹104.51.

What is usually missed in cost buildup?

Inward freight, packaging, wastage, payment-gateway or marketplace commissions, and the GST-ITC treatment are the most commonly missed cost heads.
