FAQs — अक्सर पूछे जाने वाले सवाल

HSN code kya hota hai aur invoice me kyun chahiye?

HSN (Harmonized System of Nomenclature) goods ko classify karne ka international code system hai. GST invoice me HSN code likhna mandatory hai (turnover ke hisaab se 4 ya 6 digit tak), kyunki isi se pata chalta hai ki kaunsa GST rate lagega.

SAC code kya hai?

SAC (Services Accounting Code) services ka classification code hai — jaise HSN goods ke liye, waise SAC services ke liye. Service invoice me 6-digit SAC code diya jata hai.

GST 2.0 me kaunse rates hain? (What are the GST 2.0 slabs?)

56th GST Council (September 2025) ke baad do mukhya slabs hain — 5% aur 18% — plus luxury aur sin goods (tobacco, pan masala, aerated drinks, badi gaadiyan) par 40% demerit rate. Ye 22 September 2025 se effective hai. Sone-chaandi par 3% special rate jaari hai.

Kya is finder me poora HSN tariff hai?

Nahi — ye ~100 curated common entries hain jo SMEs aam taur par bechte hain. Poora official tariff cbic-gst.gov.in par available hai. Confusion ho to CA se confirm karein, kyunki galat HSN par galat rate lag sakta hai.

Mera product list me nahi hai — kya karun?

Keyword badal kar try karein (jaise "phone" ki jagah "mobile"), ya chapter-level entry dekhein. Phir bhi na mile to official CBIC rate schedule ya apne CA se exact HSN confirm karein.
