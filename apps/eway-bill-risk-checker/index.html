Validity / वैधता

Common rejection / detention risks

    Rules used: Rule 138(10) CGST Rules (as amended by Notification 94/2020-CT, w.e.f. 1-Jan-2021): validity = 1 day per 200 km for regular cargo, 1 day per 20 km for ODC/multimodal-with-ship — the clock starts when Part B is filled. Extension possible within 8 hours before/after expiry (max 360 days total). Penalty for movement without a valid e-way bill: ₹10,000 or the tax sought to be evaded, whichever is higher (Sec 129).
    Note: intra-state thresholds vary by state (several states set ₹1–2 lakh instead of ₹50,000) — this tool uses the standard ₹50,000 inter-state threshold. Some states also notify extra exemptions. Verify on ewaybillgst.gov.in before dispatch.

    FAQs — अक्सर पूछे जाने वाले सवाल

    E-way bill kab mandatory hai? (When is an e-way bill mandatory?)

    Jab goods ki consignment value ₹50,000 se zyada ho (inter-state; kuch states me intra-state limit zyada hai) — movement shuru hone se pehle e-way bill generate karna mandatory hai (Sec 68, Rule 138).

    E-way bill ki validity kitni hoti hai? (What is the e-way bill validity?)

    Regular cargo: har 200 km (ya uske hisse) par 1 din; over-dimensional cargo: har 20 km par 1 din (Notification 94/2020, 1-Jan-2021 se). Validity Part B bharne ke baad shuru hoti hai.

    E-way bill expire ho jaye to kya karein?

    Expiry se 8 ghante pehle ya 8 ghante baad tak portal par validity extend ki ja sakti hai (reason ke saath). Window miss ho jaye to naya e-way bill banana padega.

    Bina e-way bill ke maal pakda gaya to penalty kitni hai?

    Section 129 ke tahat ₹10,000 ya evade kiye gaye tax ke barabar — jo bhi zyada ho — plus goods/vehicle detention ho sakta hai.

    Kya ye tool e-way bill generate karta hai?

    Nahi — ye sirf requirement aur risk ka checker hai. Actual e-way bill ewaybillgst.gov.in par hi generate hota hai.
