FAQs — अक्सर पूछे जाने वाले सवाल

GSTR-1 aur GSTR-3B ki due date kya hai?

Monthly filers: GSTR-1 — 11th, GSTR-3B — 20th of the following month. QRMP scheme me: quarterly GSTR-1 13th, quarterly GSTR-3B 22nd/24th (state category ke hisaab se).

CMP-08 kab file hota hai? (When is CMP-08 due?)

Har quarter ke baad wale mahine ki 18 tarikh tak — yaani 18 Apr, 18 Jul, 18 Oct, 18 Jan. Composition ka annual return GSTR-4 30 June tak (FY 2024-25 se — Notification 12/2024; usse pehle 30 April tha).

TDS deposit aur return ki dates kya hain?

TDS/TCS deposit: har mahine ki 7 tarikh tak. 24Q quarterly returns: 31 Jul, 31 Oct, 31 Jan, 31 May. Form 16: 15 June tak issue karna hota hai.

PF aur ESI kab jama hota hai?

Dono ka monthly deposit 15th of the following month tak hota hai.

Kya ye dates hamesha sahi rehti hain?

Ye standard recurring dates hain, lekin government notifications se kabhi-kabhi extend ho jati hain. Deadline ke paas official portal par zaroor re-verify karein — isiliye calendar par ye honest note diya gaya hai.
