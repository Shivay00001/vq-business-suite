Credit utilization — FAQs

What is credit line utilization?

Drawn amount ÷ sanctioned limit, as a %. High utilization (≥75–90%) signals stress to lenders, leaves no headroom, and can trigger penal charges on over-limit drawings.

क्रेडिट लाइन यूटिलाइज़ेशन क्या है?

उपयोग की गई राशि ÷ स्वीकृत सीमा। 75% से ऊपर चेतावनी, 90% से ऊपर गंभीर — हेडरूम खत्म हो जाता है और पेनल्टी लग सकती है।

How is the interest estimated?

On the drawn amount at your contracted annual rate ÷ 12. Fees, penal interest, compounding and minimum charges are not modelled — your sanction letter and statement are final.

Why track utilization across facilities?

To see total exposure, the weighted average cost of borrowed money, and which facility to repay first (usually the highest rate / highest utilization).

Where is my facility data stored?

On your own device (up to 25 facilities). Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Does this replace my bank statement?

No — update the drawn amounts from your statements monthly; this is a monitoring dashboard, not an official record.
