Due fee reminder tool — FAQs

How do I send fee reminders on WhatsApp?

Select overdue students, pick WhatsApp and a language, then copy the generated text and paste it into WhatsApp yourself. The tool never sends messages automatically.

क्या हिंदी में भी रिमाइंडर मैसेज बनता है?

हाँ — भाषा चुनने पर हिंदी या अंग्रेज़ी में WhatsApp/SMS टेम्पलेट तैयार होता है, जिसमें छात्र का नाम, कक्षा, बकाया राशि और देय तिथि अपने आप भर जाती है।

How do I know whom I already reminded?

Tick the checkbox and press "Mark selected as reminded" — a badge appears next to the student with the channel and time.

Does this tool spam parents?

No. It only drafts text for you to copy. Follow your institute policy and messaging-app rules; stop on any opt-out.
