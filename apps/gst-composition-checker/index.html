Tax estimate / टैक्स अनुमान

Formula: Composition tax = aggregate turnover × rate (1% traders/manufacturers · 5% restaurants · 6% service providers), paid quarterly via CMP-08 (18th of the month after each quarter) + GSTR-4 annual return (30th June, from FY 2024-25). Tax is on turnover, not profit — and no ITC is available.
Please confirm with your CA before opting in/out. Key trade-offs: no ITC, no tax invoice (only Bill of Supply — customers can't claim ITC on your sales), no inter-state sales, and you must exit the scheme the moment turnover crosses the limit.

FAQs — अक्सर पूछे जाने वाले सवाल

Composition scheme me turnover limit kitni hai?

Goods (traders/manufacturers/restaurants): ₹1.5 crore preceding FY turnover — lekin Arunachal Pradesh, Manipur, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Nagaland, Sikkim, Tripura aur Uttarakhand me ₹75 lakh. Service providers (Sec 10(2A)): ₹50 lakh pure India me.

Composition scheme me tax rate kitna hai? (What are the composition tax rates?)

Traders aur manufacturers 1%, restaurants (bina alcohol) 5%, eligible service providers 6% — ye rate total turnover par lagta hai, profit par nahi.

Kya composition dealer ITC claim kar sakta hai?

Nahi. Composition scheme me Input Tax Credit nahi milta, aur aap tax invoice ki jagah sirf Bill of Supply issue kar sakte hain — aapke B2B customers aapki purchase par ITC claim nahi kar payenge.

Composition me kaunsi returns file hoti hain?

CMP-08 — har quarter ke baad wale mahine ki 18 tarikh tak tax payment statement; aur GSTR-4 — saal me ek baar 30 June tak annual return (FY 2024-25 se).

Kya ye verdict final hai?

Nahi — ye eligibility ka indicative check hai, tax advice nahi. Opt-in/out se pehle apne CA se confirm zaroor karein.
