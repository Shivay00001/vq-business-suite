Backup scheduler — FAQs

What is the 3-2-1 backup rule?

Keep 3 copies of your data, on 2 different media types, with 1 copy off-site. This app checks your plan against that rule.

3-2-1 बैकअप नियम क्या है?

डेटा की 3 प्रतियां, 2 अलग-अलग मीडिया पर, 1 प्रति ऑफ-साइट। यह ऐप आपकी योजना को इस नियम पर जांचता है।

How often should a small business back up?

Daily for active accounting data (Tally, invoices); weekly is the minimum for most small businesses. Monthly alone is rarely enough.

Where is my backup plan stored?

In the on-device vault in your browser. It lists when to back up — it does not back up files itself.
