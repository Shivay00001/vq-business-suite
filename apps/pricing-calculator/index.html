FAQs — अक्सर पूछे जाने वाले सवाल

Product ka selling price kaise nikalein?

Total unit cost nikalein (material + labor + overhead + packaging + delivery), phir formula Price = Cost ÷ (1 − margin%) lagayein. 30% margin chahiye to ₹200 cost par price = ₹200 ÷ 0.70 = ₹285.71.

GST 2.0 me kaun se slabs hain? (Sept 2025 se)

GST 2.0 (22 Sept 2025 se) me 4 slabs hain: 0%, 5%, 18% aur 40%. Purane 12% aur 28% slabs khatm ho gaye. 40% sirf luxury/sin goods (tobacco, pan masala, luxury cars) par lagta hai.

GST inclusive aur exclusive me kya farak hai?

Exclusive: price par GST alag se judta hai (₹100 + 18% = ₹118). Inclusive: price me GST pehle se shamil hai — tax nikalne ke liye Price × Rate ÷ (100 + Rate) ka formula lagta hai.

10% discount margin ko kitna ghata deta hai?

Discount seedha margin kha jata hai. 30% margin wale product par 10% discount ke baad margin ~22% reh jata hai — calculator upar exact hisaab dikhata hai.

Break-even units kya hote hain?

Wo units jinhe bechne par fixed cost poori cover ho jaye: Fixed cost ÷ (Price − Variable cost per unit). Isse kam becha to nuksaan, zyada becha to munafa.
