Formula — गणना कैसे होती है

FIFO: COGS = Σ (earliest lots consumed first) — jo lot sabse pehle khareeda, sale me wahi pehle ghatta hai. Closing value = remaining lots × unke rates

Weighted average: Avg rate = Σ(Qty×Rate) ÷ ΣQty · COGS = Sold qty × Avg rate · Closing value = Closing qty × Avg rate

Example: buy 100@₹10, buy 100@₹12, sell 120 → FIFO COGS ₹1,240, closing 80@₹12 = ₹960; WAVG avg ₹11, COGS ₹1,320, closing ₹880.

FAQs — अक्सर पूछे जाने वाले सवाल

FIFO aur weighted average me kya farak hai?

FIFO me sabse purana lot pehle becha maana jaata hai (closing stock latest rates par), jabki weighted average me sabhi lots ka ausat rate lagta hai. Dono Companies Act / Income Tax me accepted methods hain.

COGS kya hota hai?

COGS (Cost of Goods Sold) = beche gaye maal ki khareed laagat. Profit = Sales − COGS.

Kaun sa method chunna chahiye?

Rising prices me FIFO closing stock zyada dikhata hai; weighted average price fluctuations ko smooth karta hai. Ek baar chuna method har saal consistent rakhein.

इन्वेंटरी वैल्यूएशन क्यों ज़रूरी है?

Balance sheet me stock ki sahi value aur profit ki sahi ganana ke liye valuation method zaroori hai — galat method se tax aur audit dono me dikkat hoti hai.
