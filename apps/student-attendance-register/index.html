Student attendance register — FAQs

How do I mark daily attendance?

Pick a date and tap Present/Absent/Leave for each student. Marks save automatically in your browser.

मासिक उपस्थिति प्रतिशत कैसे निकालें?

महीना चुनें और रिपोर्ट बनाएँ — हर छात्र की present/absent/leave संख्या और प्रतिशत अपने आप निकलेगा। 75% से कम वालों की डिफॉल्टर सूची भी मिलेगी।

Who counts as a defaulter?

Any student with attendance below your threshold (default 75%) for the selected month, provided they have at least one marked day.

Is attendance data stored online?

No — everything stays in your browser vault (max 25 students). Nothing is uploaded.
