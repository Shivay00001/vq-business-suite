FAQs — अक्सर पूछे जाने वाले सवाल

Price history track karne se kya fayda?

Rate kab badha/ghata, kaun sa supplier sasta padta hai, aur bargaining ke liye proof — ye sab price history se milta hai.

Latest vs average % ka matlab kya hai?

Ye batata hai ki aaj ka rate ausat rate se kitna % upar/neeche hai — positive % matlab abhi rate mehenga chal raha hai.

Supplier comparison kaise use karein?

Har supplier ka avg aur latest rate compare karein — consistently saste supplier se zyada order dein, mehange se bargain karein.

प्राइस हिस्ट्री क्यों ज़रूरी है?

Raw material ke bhav roz badalte hain — bina history ke pata nahi chalta kab sasta khareedein aur supplier rate sahi de raha hai ya nahi.
