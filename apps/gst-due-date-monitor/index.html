FAQs — अक्सर पूछे जाने वाले सवाल

GSTR-3B ki due date kya hai?

Monthly filers: agle mahine ki 20 tarikh. QRMP (quarterly) filers: quarter ke baad wale mahine ki 22 ya 24 tarikh (state group ke hisaab se).

GSTR-1 kab file hota hai?

Monthly: agle mahine ki 11 tarikh. QRMP: quarter ke baad wale mahine ki 13 tarikh.

Composition dealer ki due dates kya hain?

CMP-08 (quarterly tax payment): har quarter ke baad wale mahine ki 18 tarikh. CMP-08 par late fee nahi, par late tax par 18% p.a. interest lagta hai. GSTR-4 (annual): agle financial year ki 30 April.

GSTR-9 kab file karna hota hai?

Financial year ke baad 31 December tak. ₹2 crore se kam turnover par GSTR-9 optional hai; GSTR-9C ₹5 crore se zyada turnover par lagta hai.

Kya ye app mujhe reminder bhejega?

Nahi — iska koi backend/server nahi hai, isliye notifications bhejna technically possible nahi. Ye live countdown dikhata hai; page bookmark karke weekly check karein.

Late filing par kitna late fee lagega?

₹50/day (₹25 CGST + ₹25 SGST); nil return par ₹20/day. Cap: nil ₹500 · ≤₹1.5 cr ₹2,000 · ₹1.5–5 cr ₹5,000 · >₹5 cr ₹10,000.
