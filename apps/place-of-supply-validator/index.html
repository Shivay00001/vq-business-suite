Remember: place of supply decides the tax type, not whether tax applies. Same state as supplier → CGST + SGST (each half the rate); different state → full IGST. Exports are zero-rated. Unusual cases (SEZ, imports, OIDAR, e-commerce) have special rules — confirm with your CA.

FAQs — अक्सर पूछे जाने वाले सवाल

Place of supply ka matlab kya hai?

GST destination-based tax hai — tax us state ko milta hai jahan supply consume hoti hai. Place of supply wahi "destination" hai; iske hisaab se decide hota hai ki CGST+SGST lagega ya IGST.

IGST kab lagta hai aur CGST+SGST kab? (When is IGST vs CGST+SGST charged?)

Supplier ka state aur place of supply same ho → CGST + SGST (rate aadha-aadha). Alag-alag state ho → poora IGST. Ye Sections 7 (inter-state) aur 8 (intra-state), IGST Act ke tahat hai.

Services me B2B aur B2C me kya farak hai?

General rule (Sec 12(2)): B2B me place of supply recipient ka location hota hai; B2C me recipient ka address record par ho to wahi, warna supplier ka location.

Bill-to / ship-to case me place of supply kya hoga?

Sec 10(1)(b): agar supplier third person ke direction par goods deliver karta hai, to mana jata hai ki third person ne goods receive kiye — place of supply third person ke principal place of business wala state hota hai.

Kya ye tool har case cover karta hai?

Nahi — common domestic cases cover hote hain. SEZ supplies, imports, OIDAR/e-commerce aur kuch special services ke alag rules hain; doubt ho to CA se confirm karein.
