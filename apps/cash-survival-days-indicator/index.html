Cash runway — FAQs

How many days can my business survive on current cash?

Divide cash in hand by daily burn (all outflows ÷ 30). Below 30 days is critical, 30–90 is watch, above 90 is safe.

मेरा बिज़नेस कितने दिन चल सकता है?

हाथ में कैश ÷ रोज़ाना खर्च = बचे हुए दिन। 30 दिन से कम क्रिटिकल, 30–90 वॉच, 90 से ऊपर सुरक्षित।

What counts as daily burn?

Everything leaving the account: salaries, rent, EMIs, supplier payments, taxes, owner drawings — total monthly outflow ÷ 30.

My burn is zero — is my runway infinite?

Mathematically yes, but keep a 3-month buffer anyway. A zero-burn month rarely repeats.

Is this financial advice?

No — a planning indicator. Talk to your CA before borrowing or restructuring payables.
