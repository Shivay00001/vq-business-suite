FAQs — अक्सर पूछे जाने वाले सवाल

Profit and loss statement kaise banaye?

Upar apni income aur expense entries add karein (category aur date ke saath), financial year chunein — statement automatic ban jayega. Snapshot save karke print/PDF bhi le sakte hain.

Gross profit aur net profit me kya farak hai?

Gross profit = Revenue − COGS (maal ki seedhi lagat). Net profit = Gross profit − operating expenses (rent, salary, marketing aadi). Dono margins revenue ke percentage me dikhaye jate hain.

Kya entries edit ya delete ho sakti hain?

Haan — har row me Edit aur Delete button hai. Saari entries aapke browser me save rehti hain, koi account nahi chahiye.

Kya mera financial data safe hai?

Haan — data sirf aapke device par store hota hai, kahin upload nahi hota. Chahein to Vault passphrase se encrypt kar sakte hain.
