# HSN / SAC Code Finder — `hsn-sac-finder`

Searchable curated dataset of 111 common HSN chapters (goods) and SAC headings
(services) with GST 2.0 rates.

## Rates basis (verified Sep 2026)
- 56th GST Council (3-Sep-2025), effective **22-Sep-2025**: two main slabs **5%**
  and **18%**, plus **40% demerit** rate on luxury/sin goods (tobacco, pan masala,
  aerated drinks, large cars/motorcycles). The older 12%/28% slabs were largely
  merged into 5%/18%.
- Special **3%** rate on gold/silver/jewellery continues.
- Entries whose rate depends on a condition (branded vs loose, pack size, room
  tariff) carry the condition in the chapter note and show both rates.

## Statutory audit (2026-09-26)
Every rate in the dataset was spot-checked against GST 2.0 reporting
(testbook, bajajfinserv, thehindubusinessline, wionews, newindianexpress,
financialexpress, economictimes — Sep 2025 rate lists) and corrected:
hotel accommodation ≤₹7,500 → 5% (no ITC) / >₹7,500 → 18% (ITC);
marble blocks 12% → 5%; uncoated paper 12% → 18% (notebook paper nil);
mobiles 12% → 18%; tractors 12% → 5%; motorcycles >350cc → 40% demerit;
lottery/betting/casinos/horse racing → 40%; gyms/salons/yoga 18% → 5% (no ITC);
life & health insurance → nil; natural gas kept outside GST (entry renamed to
domestic LPG). GSTR-4 due date corrected to **30 June** (FY 2024-25 onwards,
Notification 12/2024) in the calendar and composition apps.

## Honest limitation
The dataset is **curated, not the full CBIC tariff** — the page says this plainly
and points to cbic-gst.gov.in for the official schedule. Do not present it as
exhaustive.
