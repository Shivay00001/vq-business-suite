FAQs — अक्सर पूछे जाने वाले सवाल

Trial balance kya hota hai?

Trial balance saare ledger accounts ke debit/credit balances ki list hai. Double-entry system me total debits hamesha total credits ke barabar hone chahiye — yehi check ye tool karta hai.

Debits credits se match na karein to kya karein?

Farak ki rakam Suspense Account me dal kar books temporarily tally ki jati hain, phir galti dhoondh kar sudhari jati hai. Ye tool exact difference aur suspense entry dono batata hai.

Kya tally hona matlab hisaab sahi hai?

Nahi. Ye tool sirf arithmetic check karta hai. Galat account me entry, chhooti hui entry, ya ulta debit/credit bhi kabhi-kabhi tally ho jata hai. Final checking CA/accountant se karwayein.

ट्रायल बैलेंस कैसे बनता है?

Har ledger account ka closing balance nikalkar — debit balance debit column me, credit balance credit column me. Dono columns ka total barabar hona chahiye.
