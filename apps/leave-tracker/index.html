Leave tracker — FAQs

How does the apply → approve flow work?

Apply creates a pending request. Approving deducts the days from that leave type's balance; rejecting leaves the balance untouched. Pending requests never reduce the balance, so an employee can't be double-charged.

छुट्टी का आवेदन और अनुमोदन कैसे काम करता है?

आवेदन करने पर अनुरोध “pending” रहता है। अनुमोदन (approve) पर दिनों की कटौती बैलेंस से होती है; अस्वीकृति (reject) पर बैलेंस नहीं घटता।

What is the negative-balance guard?

Neither applying nor approving can push a leave balance below zero — the app blocks the action and tells you how many days are actually available.

Are the CL/SL/EL quotas fixed by law?

No. Quotas depend on your state's Shops & Establishments Act and company policy (Factories Act gives ~1 EL day per 20 days worked). The defaults (12/12/15) are typical SME practice — edit them to match your policy.

Is my data private?

Yes. Nothing leaves your device; saving is locked until you set a passphrase, and records are then stored encrypted (AES-256-GCM) in this browser only.
