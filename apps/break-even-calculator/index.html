FAQs — अक्सर पूछे जाने वाले सवाल

Break-even point kaise nikale?

Break-even units = Fixed costs ÷ (Selling price − Variable cost per unit). Break-even revenue = Break-even units × Selling price. Upar values dalein — chart ke saath turant result.

Margin of safety kya hota hai?

Aapki current sales break-even se kitni upar hain: (Current sales − Break-even units) ÷ Current sales × 100. Jitna zyada, utna surakshit business.

Agar selling price variable cost se kam ho to?

Toh har unit par nuksaan hoga aur break-even kabhi nahi aayega — calculator aapko pehle price sudharne ko kahega.

What-if analysis kya batata hai?

Price 10% badhane ya ghatane par break-even kitna badlega — taaki aap pricing decisions data dekhkar lein, andaze par nahi.
