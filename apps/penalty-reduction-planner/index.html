GSTR-3B penalty — FAQs

What is the late fee for delayed GSTR-3B filing?

Rs 50/day for returns with tax liability (Rs 25 CGST + Rs 25 SGST) and Rs 20/day for nil returns — verified Sep 2026.

GSTR-3B में देरी पर कितना जुर्माना लगता है?

टैक्स वाली रिटर्न पर ₹50/दिन, निल रिटर्न पर ₹20/दिन। निल पर अधिकतम ₹500, बाकी पर जुलाई 2025 से ₹10,000 की फ्लैट कैप।

What is the maximum GSTR-3B late fee?

Rs 500 for nil returns; Rs 10,000 per return for others (flat cap from July 2025; earlier it was turnover-based: Rs 2,000 / 5,000 / 10,000).

What interest applies on delayed GST payment?

18% per annum on the delayed net cash tax liability (after ITC), computed per day of delay.

Should I pay now or wait?

Almost always pay now — the fee grows Rs 50/day and unpaid fees block your future return filings. This tool shows the exact extra cost of waiting.

Is this official tax advice?

No — a planning aid. Confirm the final amount on the GST portal and with your CA before filing.
