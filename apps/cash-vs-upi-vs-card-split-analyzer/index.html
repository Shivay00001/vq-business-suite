Sales mix & reconciliation — FAQs

What is fee drag in a sales mix?

The % of your sales lost to payment acceptance fees — MDR on cards and UPI, zero on cash. A 2% drag on ₹10 lakh monthly sales is ₹20,000/month leaking to fees.

सेल्स मिक्स में फ़ी ड्रैग क्या है?

भुगतान शुल्क में जाने वाली बिक्री का प्रतिशत। ₹10 लाख की बिक्री पर 2% ड्रैग = ₹20,000/माह का नुकसान।

How fast does each mode settle?

Cash: same day. UPI: usually same day or T+1. Cards: T+1/T+2 via the acquirer, credited net of MDR.

Why don't card credits match my sales report?

Settlements arrive NET of MDR and may deduct refunds/chargebacks. Reconcile gross sale vs net credit; the gap should equal your MDR %.

How do I reconcile UPI sales?

Match the day's UPI-app/POS report against bank credits within 2 days, and confirm your QR's P2M vs P2PM classification for the correct MDR.

Are the fee rates fixed?

No — editable Sep-2026 benchmarks (UPI 0.25% blended, cards 2.0% blended). Your acquirer's settlement statement is final.
