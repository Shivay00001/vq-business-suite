Suggested order = Reorder point − Current stock (0 se kam nahi). Isse naya order lead-time demand + safety buffer cover karta hai.

Formula — गणना कैसे होती है

Avg daily sales = Total sold qty ÷ History ke din (history na ho to manual value)

Reorder point = (Avg daily sales × Lead time days) + Safety stock

Suggested order qty = max(0, Reorder point − Current stock) (round up)

Days of stock left = Current stock ÷ Avg daily sales

Example: avg 5/day, lead 7 days, safety 15, stock 20 → ROP = 5×7+15 = 50, suggested order = 50−20 = 30.

FAQs — अक्सर पूछे जाने वाले सवाल

Reorder point kya hota hai?

Woh stock level jahan pahunchte hi naya order kar dena chahiye — formula: (Avg daily sales × Lead time) + Safety stock.

Safety stock kyun rakhein?

Supplier delay ya achanak demand badhne par stockout se bachne ke liye extra buffer — bina iske har delay me sale rukti hai.

Avg daily sales kaise nikle?

Sales history darj karein — app total sold qty ko history ke dino se divide karke avg nikaalta hai. History na ho to manual value bhi daal sakte hain.

स्टॉक फोरकास्ट से क्या फायदा है?

Sahi time par sahi quantity ka order — na stockout ka nuksaan, na overstocking me fasa paisa. Chhote business ke liye ye sabse sasta planning tool hai.
