Assumptions (all editable above)

  • PF: 12% employee + 12% employer on Basic (+DA), wage ceiling ₹25,000/month (₹3,000 each max), w.e.f. 17-09-2026 (S.O. 5109(E)). Employer share = 8.33% pension (EPS) + 3.67% PF.
  • ESI: 0.75% employee + 3.25% employer on gross salary, only when gross ≤ ₹21,000/month (₹25,000 for persons with disabilities).
  • Professional tax: deducted per your state's monthly slab (e.g. Maharashtra ₹200/month above ₹10,000; ₹300 in February).
  • Gratuity: (Basic + DA) ÷ 26 × 15 per year, shown as employer cost inside CTC (payable after 5 years of service).
  • Special allowance is the balancing figure: gross − Basic − HRA − DA.
  • New regime tax: FY 2025-26 slabs 0/5/10/15/20/25/30% on 0–4L/4–8L/8–12L/12–16L/16–20L/20–24L/>24L; ₹75,000 standard deduction; zero tax up to ₹12L taxable (87A rebate, marginal relief just above).
  • Old regime tax: slabs 0/5/20/30% on 0–2.5L/2.5–5L/5–10L/>10L; ₹50,000 standard deduction; 80C (₹1.5L max incl. PF), 80D, HRA exemption and PT deducted; 87A rebate up to ₹5L.
  • Cess: 4% health & education cess on tax + surcharge (surcharge applies above ₹50L).
  • Employees with average daily wage ≤ ₹176 are exempt from the employee ESI share — not modelled here.

Salary calculator — FAQs

How is in-hand salary calculated from CTC in India?

Gross salary = CTC minus employer PF (12% of Basic up to ₹25,000/month, w.e.f. 17-09-2026), employer ESI (3.25% if gross is within ₹21,000/month) and gratuity accrual. In-hand = gross minus employee PF (12%), employee ESI (0.75%), professional tax and income-tax TDS.

What is the PF deduction on salary?

12% of Basic + DA from the employee, matched by 12% from the employer, on wages up to ₹25,000/month (₹3,000/month each), w.e.f. 17-09-2026 (S.O. 5109(E)). The employer's share splits into 8.33% pension (EPS) and 3.67% PF.

What is the ESI deduction rate?

0.75% of gross salary from the employee and 3.25% from the employer, applicable when gross monthly wages are ₹21,000 or less (₹25,000 for persons with disabilities).

Which tax regime is better in FY 2025-26?

It depends on your deductions. The new regime has lower slab rates, a ₹75,000 standard deduction and zero tax up to ₹12 lakh taxable income; the old regime allows 80C, 80D, HRA and other deductions with a ₹50,000 standard deduction. This calculator shows both side by side.
