What-if scenario simulator — FAQs

What is a what-if scenario analysis?

You define a base case and alternative scenarios (optimistic/pessimistic) over price, volume, unit cost and fixed cost, and compare the resulting profit side by side instead of guessing.

व्हाट-इफ़ सिनेरियो एनालिसिस क्या है?

बेस केस और वैकल्पिक सिनेरियो (आशावादी/निराशावादी) में कीमत, वॉल्यूम, यूनिट लागत और फिक्स्ड लागत बदलकर मुनाफ़े की तुलना करें।

How does the profit waterfall work?

The profit difference between two scenarios is split exactly into four additive effects: price effect (Δp × base volume), volume effect ((new price − base cost) × Δvolume), cost effect (−Δcost × new volume) and fixed-cost effect (−Δfixed). They sum to the total difference.

Which lever moves profit most?

Usually price: every ₹1 of price flows almost fully to profit, while a ₹1 cost change is diluted. The waterfall shows each lever's exact rupee contribution for your numbers.

Is this a financial forecast?

No — it is a deterministic comparison of scenarios you define. It does not model probability, seasonality, or demand elasticity.
