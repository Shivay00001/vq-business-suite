Exit settlement — FAQs

What goes into a full & final settlement?

Unpaid salary for days worked, leave encashment for unused earned leave, gratuity (if eligible), pro-rata bonus, notice pay — minus advances/loans, TDS and other deductions. The net is the amount payable to the employee.

How is leave encashment calculated?

There is no single statutory formula for all establishments; this calculator uses the common convention (Basic ÷ 26) × unused earned-leave days and states it on-screen. Some companies use gross ÷ 30 — use whichever your policy specifies.

Is leave encashment taxable?

Yes, generally — leave encashment received on resignation/termination is taxable (government employees get an exemption up to a limit). Gratuity above ₹20 lakh is also taxable. Confirm TDS with your CA.
