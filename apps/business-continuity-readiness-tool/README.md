# Business Continuity Readiness Tool — `business-continuity-readiness-tool`

12-item BCP checklist (backups, tested restores, offsite copy, key-person
cover, alternate site, insurance, cash reserve…) with weights → readiness
% and Resilient/Developing/Exposed tier, gaps sorted heaviest-first.
Assessments savable to on-device vault (max 25).

## Free limits
20 assessments/day (metered). Vault saves capped at 25.

## Honest limitation
Self-assessment estimate, not a certified BCP audit. Confirm with your
auditor/insurer.
