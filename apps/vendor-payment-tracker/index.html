Vendor payment tracker — FAQs

What is the MSME 45-day payment rule?

Under Section 15 of the MSMED Act 2006, a buyer must pay an MSME supplier within the agreed credit period, which cannot exceed 45 days from acceptance of goods/services — even if the contract says longer.

MSME 45-दिन का भुगतान नियम क्या है?

MSMED एक्ट 2006 की धारा 15 के तहत खरीदार को MSME सप्लायर को स्वीकृति के 45 दिनों के भीतर भुगतान करना होता है — अनुबंध में ज़्यादा अवधि लिखी हो तो भी।

What interest applies on delayed MSME payments?

Section 16 mandates compound interest with monthly rests at three times the RBI bank rate. With the bank rate at 5.50% (verified Sep 2026), that is 16.50% p.a. Confirm the current rate with your CA before filing a claim.

MSME भुगतान में देरी पर कितना ब्याज लगता है?

धारा 16 के तहत मासिक चक्रवृद्धि के साथ RBI बैंक दर के तीन गुने की दर से ब्याज — बैंक दर 5.50% (सितंबर 2026 सत्यापित) पर यह 16.50% वार्षिक है।

How does this tracker flag breaches?

Tick "MSME-registered supplier" and the agreed credit is capped at 45 days. Invoices still unpaid beyond that are flagged "MSME 45-day breach" with an interest estimate; all overdue invoices are listed with days overdue.

Where can an MSME complain about delayed payment?

On the government MSME Samadhaan portal, which routes the case to the Micro and Small Enterprises Facilitation Council (MSEFC) for conciliation and arbitration under Section 18.
